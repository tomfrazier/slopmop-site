The slop glossary

33 words for the junk that fills feeds and inboxes, from content farms and clickbait to workslop and AI slop, plus the nine tells Slop Mop looks for. Each has its own page you can share.

ABCDEFHLMNPSTVW

Nothing matches that. Try a shorter word.

A

  • Agent FOMOFear your agents are accomplishing nothing without you.
  • AgentsomniaLosing sleep worrying about your autonomous agents.
  • AI addiction also chatbot addiction, problematic AI useWhen the chatbot becomes your life.
  • AI creamThe rare AI-assisted content actually worth reading.
  • AI fatigueExhaustion from the endless AI avalanche.
  • AI genieA machine that gives you whatever you ask for.
  • AI psychosis also chatbot psychosis, AI-associated psychosisA reported phenomenon, not a formal diagnosis.
  • AI shamingAccusing someone of using AI.
  • AI slapForwarding AI output you never actually read.
  • AI slop also slopMass-produced digital garbage.
  • AI suspicion also AI-dar, Turing tingleYour bullshit detector activating.

B

  • Bragawatts also braggawatts, brag-a-wattsMeasuring AI infrastructure announcements in units of hype.
  • Brain rot also brainrotToo much digital junk.
  • BroligarchyBillionaire tech bros running the show.

C

  • ChatfishingOutsourcing your dating personality to AI.
  • Cheapfake also cheap fake, shallowfakeLow-tech manipulation.
  • Counterfeit peopleMachines impersonating humans.

D

  • Dead internet theoryBots talking to bots.
  • Deepfake also deep fakeFabricated audio or video.

E

  • Empty praisetellEvaluation with no detail behind it.
  • Engagement baittellAsking for comments, reposts and follows.
  • EnshittificationPlatforms systematically getting worse.

F

  • Feel the AGIThe AI industry’s religious experience.
  • Flowery prosetellFlourish instead of the plain claim.

H

  • Hype wordstellMarketing vocabulary.

L

  • Liar's dividendDismissing genuine evidence as fake.

M

  • Meat proxy also meat-based LLM proxyHuman who forwards AI output without checking it.

N

  • Nerd ReichTech authoritarianism with venture funding.
  • No-catch promisestellAll benefit, no cost.
  • “Not X, but Y”tellDefining things by what they aren't.

P

  • Parasocial also para-social, parasocial relationship, parasocial interactionOne-sided emotional relationships.
  • P(doom) also p(doom), pdoom, probability of doomAssigning a probability to humanity’s extinction.
  • PromptatoryEndless cycles of fixing AI-generated mistakes.

S

  • Scroll-stoppertellDramatic hook, one-line paragraphs, a moral.
  • SlopagandaPropaganda at slop prices.
  • Stiff phrasingtellStock transitions and hedges.

T

  • TasteslopCuration with the taste taken out.
  • Technofeudalism also techno-feudalism, digital feudalism, neo-feudalismWelcome back to the Middle Ages, now with GPUs.
  • Too-tidy storytellAn anecdote that exists to prove the moral.

V

  • Vibe coding also vibe-coding, vibecodingProgramming without reading the code.
  • Vibe revenue also curiosity revenueValuation fuel that hasn’t bothered becoming revenue.

W

  • WorkslopPolished work that hands the actual work back to you.
