The slop glossary

92 words for the junk that fills feeds and inboxes, from content farms and clickbait to workslop and AI slop, plus the nine tells Slop Mop looks for. Each has its own page you can share.

Many of these terms, from AI slap and meat proxy to agentsomnia and AI cream, were gathered by Oren Etzioni and Daniel S. Weld in “The colorful and cranky language of the AI era, from slop to slap” (GeekWire, 16 October 2026). Each page credits the term’s own origin too. New to the idea? Start with AI slop, the nine tells and how Slop Mop works.

ABCDEFHIKLMNOPRSTVWY

Nothing matches that. Try a shorter word.

A

  • Agent FOMOFear your agents are accomplishing nothing without you.
  • AgentsomniaLosing sleep worrying about your autonomous agents.
  • AI addiction also chatbot addiction, problematic AI useWhen the chatbot becomes your life.
  • AI creamThe rare AI-assisted content actually worth reading.
  • AI fatigueExhaustion from the endless AI avalanche.
  • AI genieA machine that gives you whatever you ask for.
  • AI psychosis also chatbot psychosis, AI-associated psychosisA reported phenomenon, not a formal diagnosis.
  • AI shamingAccusing someone of using AI.
  • AI slapForwarding AI output you never actually read.
  • AI slop also slopMass-produced digital garbage.
  • AI snake oilAI sold to do things AI can't do.
  • AI suspicion also AI-dar, Turing tingleYour bullshit detector activating.
  • AI sycophancy also sycophancyA yes-man with a very large vocabulary.
  • AI washingGreenwashing, but the leaves are a chatbot.
  • Article spinning also content spinning, spinning, RogetingThe same article, wearing a thesaurus.
  • Astroturfing also astroturfGrass roots, made in a factory.

B

  • BacnEmail you asked for and never read.
  • BotshitA hallucination with a human signature.
  • Bragawatts also braggawatts, brag-a-wattsMeasuring AI infrastructure announcements in units of hype.
  • Brain rot also brainrotToo much digital junk.
  • Brandolini’s law also bullshit asymmetry principleTen minutes to make it, three days to debunk it.
  • Broetry also broem, broemsSelf-help, one line at a time.
  • BroligarchyBillionaire tech bros running the show.
  • Bullshit (Frankfurt) also Frankfurtian bullshitSaying whatever works, true or not.

C

  • ChatfishingOutsourcing your dating personality to AI.
  • Cheapfake also cheap fake, shallowfakeLow-tech manipulation.
  • Chumbox also chum box, chumThe grid of weird ads below the article.
  • ChurnalismThe press release, now with a byline.
  • ClankerA droid slur that found a second career.
  • Click farmPopularity, sold by the thousand.
  • Clickbait also click-bait, link bait, linkbaitA headline that writes cheques the article can't cash.
  • Cognitive debtLet the chatbot think now, pay for it later.
  • Content farm also content mill, AI content farmArticles grown for search engines, harvested by ads.
  • Content shockInfinite posts, one pair of eyes.
  • CopypastaThe internet's oldest reply: paste.
  • Counterfeit peopleMachines impersonating humans.

D

  • Dead internet theoryBots talking to bots.
  • Deepfake also deep fakeFabricated audio or video.
  • Delve (AI marker) also delvingThe word that gave the chatbots away.
  • Doomscrolling also doomscrollScrolling the bad news until 1am.
  • Doorway page also doorway abuse, bridge page, gateway pageA front door that opens onto another front door.

E

  • ElsagateNursery rhymes reverse-engineered from search terms.
  • Empty praisetellEvaluation with no detail behind it.
  • Engagement baittellAsking for comments, reposts and follows.
  • EnshittificationPlatforms systematically getting worse.

F

  • Feel the AGIThe AI industry’s religious experience.
  • Firehose of falsehoodLie fast, lie often, never mind consistency.
  • Flood the zone also flood the zone with shitSo much noise that the signal gives up.
  • Flowery prosetellFlourish instead of the plain claim.

H

  • Hallucination (AI) also AI hallucination, confabulationConfidently wrong, with citations.
  • Hot takeMaximum heat, minimum research.
  • Humblebrag also humble-brag, humblebraggingBragging, wearing a modest little hat.
  • Hustle pornBragging about the sleep you didn't get.
  • Hype wordstellMarketing vocabulary.

I

  • InfodemicAn outbreak of information, some of it wrong.
  • Information overload also infobesity, infoxication, information anxietyToo much to read, so you understand less.
  • Information pollution also info pollution, infollutionLitter, but for your attention.
  • Italian brainrot also Italian brain rotA shark in trainers, narrated by a robot nonno.

K

  • Keyword stuffingKeyword stuffing, keyword stuffing, keyword stuffing.

L

  • Liar's dividendDismissing genuine evidence as fake.
  • LinkedIn Lunatics also r/LinkedInLunaticsLinkedIn's greatest hits, read aloud by Reddit.
  • ListicleOne idea, cut into 10 numbered pieces.
  • LLM groomingWriting propaganda for an audience of crawlers.

M

  • Made for AdSense also MFA site, made-for-advertising siteA website whose only reader is the ad server.
  • Meat proxy also meat-based LLM proxyHuman who forwards AI output without checking it.
  • Model collapse also Habsburg AI, AI inbreeding, AI cannibalism, model autophagy disorder, MADAI eating its own leftovers.

N

  • Nerd ReichTech authoritarianism with venture funding.
  • No-catch promisestellAll benefit, no cost.
  • “Not X, but Y”tellDefining things by what they aren't.

O

  • Outrage porn also outrage media, outrage journalismAnger, served hot, for clicks.

P

  • Parasocial also para-social, parasocial relationship, parasocial interactionOne-sided emotional relationships.
  • P(doom) also p(doom), pdoom, probability of doomAssigning a probability to humanity’s extinction.
  • Pink-slime journalism also pink slimeLocal news, minus the news, plus an agenda.
  • PromptatoryEndless cycles of fixing AI-generated mistakes.

R

  • Rage bait also ragebait, rage-baiting, rage farmingAnger, farmed for engagement.
  • Reply guyFirst in the replies, last to read the post.

S

  • Scaled content abuse also mass-produced contentGoogle's name for content by the tonne.
  • Scraper site also blog scrapingSomeone else's article, now with more ads.
  • Scroll-stoppertellDramatic hook, one-line paragraphs, a moral.
  • Shitposting also shitpostLow effort, high reaction, on purpose.
  • Shrimp JesusThe patron saint of Facebook AI slop.
  • Site reputation abuse also parasite SEOCasino reviews on a university website.
  • SlopagandaPropaganda at slop prices.
  • Slopper also sloppersAsks the chatbot what to order. Every time.
  • SlopsquattingMalware waiting at the address AI made up.
  • Sludge content also content sludge, overstimulation videosFamily Guy on top, Subway Surfers underneath.
  • Sock puppet also sockpuppet, sock puppet account, sockOne person, many accounts, fake applause.
  • Spam blog also splog, auto blog, autoblogA blog with no writer and no readers.
  • Stiff phrasingtellStock transitions and hedges.
  • Stochastic parrot also stochastic parrotsFluent, confident, and repeating what it heard.

T

  • TasteslopCuration with the taste taken out.
  • Technofeudalism also techno-feudalism, digital feudalism, neo-feudalismWelcome back to the Middle Ages, now with GPUs.
  • Too-tidy storytellAn anecdote that exists to prove the moral.
  • Troll farm also troll factoryOutrage, staffed in shifts.

V

  • Vagueposting also vaguebooking, vaguebookSomething happened. No, I won't say what.
  • Vibe coding also vibe-coding, vibecodingProgramming without reading the code.
  • Vibe revenue also curiosity revenueValuation fuel that hasn’t bothered becoming revenue.

W

  • Wireborn also wirebornsThe chatbot its user believes has come alive.
  • Word saladSounds like a sentence. Means nothing.
  • WorkslopPolished work that hands the actual work back to you.

Y

  • Your AI Slop Bores Me also YASBM, youraislopbores.meAsk the chatbot. A stranger with 75 seconds answers.
