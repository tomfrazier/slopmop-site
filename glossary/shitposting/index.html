Shitposting

Low effort, high reaction, on purpose.

What shitposting means

Shitposting is posting something deliberately absurd, pointless or provocative, usually with a straight face. Merriam-Webster defines shitpost as "to post something online (such as a comment, video, or meme) that is deliberately absurd, provocative, or offensive".

The defining feature is intent. A shitpost is bad on purpose, and the people posting it know it. The Daily Dot described it in 2016 as "a deliberate provocation designed for maximum impact with minimum effort". The Financial Times called it posting "ostentatiously inane and contextless content" in a way that derails discussion.

Motives vary. Some shitposting is play: a running joke in a group chat or a surreal meme. Some is meant to drown a conversation, or, in coordinated bursts, to make a forum unusable for its regulars. The same word covers a harmless joke and a hostile flood, which is part of why it's slippery.

Where shitposting came from

Merriam-Webster finds the verb in 1993 and the noun in 1996. The current ironic sense took shape in the mid-2000s on image boards such as 4chan. Writing for Polygon, Sam Greszes compared it to Dada's absurd, context-free art, with the goal of confusing an audience until they laugh.

Its political use came to wide attention during the 2016 US presidential election, when commentators described groups using memes and mockery as a deliberate tactic. By 2024 the word had spread far enough for the Pitchfork writer Kieran Press-Reynolds to coin "shitpost modernism" for music shaped by the same sensibility.

Examples

Shitposting is usually short, and its point is the mismatch with the setting:

example

A thread in a gardening forum asks how deep to plant tulip bulbs. Reply 14, posted by an account that has never mentioned gardening, is a photo of a single potato on a dinner plate captioned "found the issue". Reply 15 is the same photo, upside down.

example

A long, serious LinkedIn post about quarterly planning gets the comment: "Agreed. This is why I only make decisions during a full moon."

Whether either is funny depends on the room. In a forum that enjoys the joke it's part of the culture; in a thread where someone needs an answer it's noise.

Copypasta

Rage bait

Online content deliberately designed to make people angry so that they comment, share and keep it circulating.

Glossary

Reply guy

Someone, usually a man, who compulsively replies to other people's posts in an unwanted, overfamiliar or condescending way.

Glossary

Vagueposting

Posting something cryptic with the context left out, so readers have to ask what happened or what it means.

Brain rot

Slang for the supposed dulling of your mind from too much trivial online content. Oxford's 2024 Word of the Year.
