To quieten a Facebook group, open the group, choose **Manage notifications** from its menu and switch in-app notifications from **Highlights** to **Friends' posts** or **Off**. That stops the alerts. To stop its posts reaching your feed as well, choose **Unfollow group**; to be done with it, **Leave group**. Nobody in the group is told about any of these.

## Before you start

- **Notifications and feed are separate.** Turning notifications off doesn't remove the group's posts from your feed, and unfollowing doesn't change your notifications. Most noisy groups need both.
- **The default is Highlights.** When you join a group, Facebook sets its notifications to Highlights, which includes suggested posts as well as posts from your friends ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/187225274663021)). That's why a group you joined once for a jumble sale can still buzz your phone.
- **Admins have extra screens.** If you run the group, the notification settings sit under Admin home. Steps for that are below.

## 1. Change a group's notifications

**On a computer:**

1. Go to facebook.com/groups and select the group.
2. Click the three dots at the top right, below the group's cover photo.
3. Select **Manage notifications**.
4. Under in-app notifications, choose a level (see the table below). Push notifications and member request notifications have their own settings on the same screen.

**In the app:**

1. Tap the menu at the top left, then **Groups**, then **Your groups**, and pick the group. If you don't see Groups, tap **See more**.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right of the group and choose **Manage notifications**.
3. Choose what you want for in-app notifications, push notifications and batch mentions.
4. Tap **Go back** to save.

If you're an admin, tap **Admin home** instead, scroll to **Personal settings** and select **Manage notifications** ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/187225274663021)).

### Which level to choose

| Level | You're notified when | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| All posts | Anyone posts | A small group where every post matters |
| Highlights | Suggested posts and friends' posts | Rarely the right choice |
| Friends' posts | A friend posts | A big local group where you know a few people |
| Off | Nobody | Anything you check when you feel like it |

([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/187225274663021))

The labels above are from Facebook's desktop instructions. The app shows the same choices, but the wording may differ slightly.

## 2. Stop @everyone notifications

Tagging @everyone notifies the whole group. In groups of up to 100 members anyone can use it; in groups of 100 to 200,000 only admins and moderators can, and each person can use it once a day in a group ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/3331417347118786)). On a computer you can turn these alerts off from the notification itself:

1. Click **Notifications** at the top right of Facebook.
2. Find the notification about everyone being tagged in that group.
3. Click the three dots next to it.
4. Choose the option to turn off @everyone notifications from that group.

This works one group at a time ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/187225274663021)). Facebook's help page only describes this on a computer; we couldn't verify an app equivalent.

## 3. Unfollow the group to keep it out of your feed

Unfollowing keeps you a member, but the group's posts stop appearing in your feed.

1. Go to the group.
2. Tap or click **Joined**.
3. Choose **Unfollow group**.

You can also do it from any of the group's posts in your feed: three dots, then **Unfollow [group name]** ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/190078864497547)). For a break rather than a permanent change, **Snooze [group name] for 30 days** in the same menu hides its posts for a month ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/538433456491590)). The [snooze and unfollow guide](/guides/facebook/facebook-snooze-unfollow-not-interested/) covers undoing both.

## 4. Leave the group

**On a computer:** go to facebook.com/groups, select the group, click **Joined** at the top right, choose **Leave group** and confirm.

**In the app:** open the group from **Menu**, **Groups**, **Your groups**, tap **Joined** below the group's name, tap **Leave Group** and confirm.

When you leave, members aren't notified, you stop getting its notifications and its posts in your feed, and the group drops off your list ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/172944012764072)). If you're the only admin, Facebook asks you to invite a new one first, and pauses the group if nobody accepts within seven days.

## Sorting all your groups in one sitting

Open **Your groups** and go down the list. For each one, decide which of four piles it belongs in:

1. **Need it now** (school, street, club): keep notifications on **All posts** or **Friends' posts**, and keep following.
2. **Useful sometimes** (a buy-and-sell group, a hobby): notifications **Off**, keep following, or check it through the Groups filter in Feeds. The [Feeds and Favorites guide](/guides/facebook/facebook-feeds-tab-favourites/) explains how.
3. **Archive only** (a past holiday, an old job): notifications **Off** and **Unfollow group**.
4. **Never again**: leave.

Groups that post mostly polls and "type YES" threads usually belong in pile 3 or 4. The [group engagement bait guide](/guides/facebook/facebook-group-engagement-bait/) covers how to tell.

:::slop
Good morning group! 🌞 Tell us one word that describes your mood today! Let's hit 1,000 comments!
:::
:::fine
Bin collections move to Friday next week because of the bank holiday. Garden waste is suspended until March.
:::

A group that sends the second is worth a notification. A group that sends the first every morning isn't.

## If that doesn't work

- **The group still notifies you.** Check push notifications on the same Manage notifications screen; they're set separately from in-app ones.
- **The Groups tab keeps showing red dots.** In the app, press and hold that tab on the tab bar and choose **Turn off notification dots** ([Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/2741283262582983)).
- **Posts from groups you're not in still appear.** Those are suggestions, or friends' activity. The [suggested posts guide](/guides/facebook/facebook-reduce-suggested-posts/) covers them.
