On X, muted words hide posts containing a word, phrase, hashtag, emoji or @username from your Home timeline and notifications. In the app, go to Notifications, tap the gear icon, then Muted, then Muted words, and tap Add; on the web the list sits under Settings and privacy. Pick where it applies and for how long.

## What muted words on X do

According to X's help centre, you can mute words, phrases, usernames, emojis and hashtags, and the mute removes matching posts from your Home timeline and notifications ([X Help Center](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/advanced-x-mute-options)). X also says it won't recommend content with your muted words, in the app or by email.

There are limits worth knowing before you start:

- **Search still shows them.** Muted words apply to your Home timeline and notifications. If you search for a muted term, you'll see the posts.
- **Muting an @username mutes mentions.** Adding @someone to the list hides posts that mention them. It doesn't mute the account itself; that's a separate control on their profile or post menu.
- **Plan for variants.** X's help page doesn't promise that a mute on "giveaway" also catches "give away" or "giving away", so add the spellings you care about as separate entries.

## Before you start

Decide what you're trying to remove. Muted words work best on phrases that almost only appear in posts you don't want. A mute on a common word ("AI", "election", "football") will hide good posts along with the bad ones, which is fine for a 24-hour break and costly as a permanent setting.

## Add a muted word in the app

1. Open the **Notifications** tab.
2. Tap the **gear icon** at the top.
3. Tap **Muted**, then **Muted words**.
4. Tap **Add**.
5. Type the word, phrase, hashtag, emoji or @username. One entry per mute.
6. Choose where it applies: **Home timeline**, **Notifications**, or both.
7. If you include notifications, you may see an option to mute matching notifications from anyone or only from people you don't follow. The labels may differ on your device.
8. Choose a duration: **Forever** (the default), **24 hours**, **7 days** or **30 days**.
9. Save the entry. The button may be labelled differently on your device.

## Add a muted word on the web

On x.com the same list lives in settings rather than under Notifications.

1. Click **More** in the left-hand menu, then **Settings and privacy**.
2. Click **Privacy and safety**, then **Mute and block**.
3. Click **Muted words**, then the add button.
4. Enter the term and choose the same options as above.

X's own instructions focus on the app, so on the web the menu may be labelled differently.

## A starter list of X muted words for bait and filler

These phrases turn up mostly in posts built to collect reactions. Add the ones that match what you keep seeing, set them to Home timeline, and leave them for 30 days before deciding whether to make them permanent.

| Mute this | It mostly catches |
|---|---|
| `follow everyone who` | follow-back promises |
| `wrong answers only` | formula questions |
| `drop yours below` | fill-in-the-blank lists |
| `unpopular opinion` | manufactured controversy |
| `nobody is talking about` | [scroll-stopper](/patterns/scroll-stopper/) hooks |
| `link in replies` | bait-and-switch hooks |
| `repost if` | [engagement bait](/patterns/engagement-bait/) |
| `game-changer` | [hype words](/patterns/hype-words/) |

Each one has costs. "Unpopular opinion" occasionally introduces a genuinely unusual view, and "link in replies" is sometimes used by people who simply want the link out of the main post. If you notice you're missing things, remove the entry.

:::slop
Unpopular opinion: consistency beats motivation. Agree? Drop yours below 👇
:::
:::fine
Unpopular with my own team, at least: we've stopped estimating tickets. Cycle time hasn't changed in three months and planning meetings are 40 minutes shorter.
:::

A mute on "unpopular opinion" would hide both of these. That's the trade. If the second kind matters to you, mute "drop yours below" instead, which catches the first post and spares the second.

## Mute for a day, not forever

The short durations are the most underused setting. When one event dominates the feed, such as a product launch, a match or a celebrity row, mute its name and hashtag for 24 hours or 7 days. You'll get the timeline back without permanently losing the topic. X lets you pick the duration per entry, so a permanent list of bait phrases and a short-lived list of news items can sit side by side.

## Muted words or Not interested

The two controls do different jobs. Muted words are precise and predictable: a phrase on the list is hidden from your Home timeline, every time, until the mute expires. Not interested is a hint to the recommendation system, so its effect is gradual and you can't see exactly what it changed. Use muted words for phrases you can name, and Not interested for the posts you can't describe in a few words.

## Review and unmute

Go back to **Muted words** using the same path. Open an entry to change its duration or scope, or remove it; X's help page says you can view and unmute words from this list. A quick review once a month keeps the list from quietly hiding things you'd now like to see.
