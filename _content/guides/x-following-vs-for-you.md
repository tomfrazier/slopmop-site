On X, the For You tab mixes posts from accounts you follow with posts X recommends, while the Following tab shows only posts from accounts you follow. Switch between them with the two tabs at the top of Home, in the app or on x.com; X remembers the last one you used. If most of your noise comes from strangers, stay in Following and spend ten minutes tidying who you follow.

## Following vs For You on X: what each tab shows

X's help centre describes the two tabs this way ([X Help Center](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-timeline)):

| | For you | Following |
|---|---|---|
| Whose posts | Accounts you follow, plus recommended posts from accounts you don't | Only accounts you follow |
| Shaped by | Your activity and feedback, including Not interested | Who you follow |
| Good for | Finding new accounts and topics | Keeping up with people you chose |
| Main risk | Recommended bait and pile-ons | Missing things outside your bubble |

X's page on recommendations confirms that posts from accounts you don't follow can appear in your Home timeline, and that feedback such as "Not interested in this post" is used as a signal ([X Help Center](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/recommendations)). That's why For You can feel busier than the list of people you actually chose.

## How to switch

### In the app (iOS and Android)

1. Open the **Home** tab, the house icon.
2. At the top of the screen, tap **Following**. Tap **For you** to go back.
3. You can also swipe left and right across the top of the timeline to move between tabs.

### On the web

1. Go to x.com and click **Home** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click **Following** at the top of the timeline. Click **For you** to switch back.

X reopens the tab you last used, so if you choose Following and leave it there, that's what you'll see next time ([X Help Center](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-timeline)). X's help centre doesn't describe a setting that removes the For You tab altogether.

## Following or For You on X: which one to use

**Use Following when** you're on X to keep up with specific people: colleagues, reporters, a sports club, writers you like. You'll see less [engagement bait](/patterns/engagement-bait/) from strangers, because there aren't any strangers.

**Use For You when** you want to find new accounts or see what's being discussed beyond your own follows. Treat it as somewhere to visit for a few minutes at a time.

**During breaking news**, Following is usually the calmer choice. For You mixes in recommended posts from accounts you've never checked, and in the first hours of a big story those can include a lot of speculation and pile-ons. In Following you'll see the story through accounts you already trust, at the pace they post it. If you need wider coverage, a List of reporters and official sources does that job without the recommendations.

A pattern that works for many readers: Following by default, For You for five minutes when you want something new, and a follow (or a List) for anything good you find there, so you'll see it again in Following.

## Tidy your Following tab

Following is only as good as the accounts in it. After years on the platform, many people follow hundreds of accounts they wouldn't choose today.

1. Open your profile and tap or click **Following**, the count under your name, to see the list. The layout may differ slightly between the app and the web.
2. Scroll through it. For each account you don't recognise or wouldn't miss, open the profile and look at their last ten posts.
3. Unfollow if most of those posts are bait, formula threads or reposts of other people's bait.
4. Repeat for 20 accounts a day rather than all at once; it's easier to judge fairly.

Questions worth asking about each account:

- Do they post things I've used, quoted or learned from?
- Is their timeline mostly their own words or mostly reposts?
- Would I follow them if I came across them today?

:::slop
GM 🌅 Who's building today? Reply with what you're working on 👇
:::
:::fine
Our planning committee meets Thursday at 7pm. The agenda includes the cycle lane on Mill Road; papers are on the council site under item 6.
:::

An account whose last ten posts look like the first is filling your Following tab with prompts for replies. An account that posts the second kind is the reason to use Following at all.

## Topic tabs for Premium subscribers

In April 2026 X began letting Premium subscribers pin topic-based timelines next to For You and Following. TechCrunch and MacRumors reported that it launched first in the iOS app, with Android to follow, and that X uses Grok to label posts by topic rather than matching keywords ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/22/hands-on-with-xs-new-ai-powered-custom-feeds/), [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/22/x-custom-timelines-for-premium-users/)). If you have Premium on iOS, look for an add button beside the Following tab; the label may differ. These tabs are still recommendations, only narrower, so the same bait can turn up inside them.

## If that doesn't work

If Following is still noisy after a tidy, the next step is a List: a hand-picked set of accounts you can pin beside your Home tabs. X's help centre says a pinned List appears to the right of For You and Following ([X Help Center](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-lists)). For trimming For You itself, see [how to clean up your For You timeline](/guides/x/x-clean-up-for-you-timeline/), and for phrases you'd rather never see in either tab, [how to use muted words on X](/guides/x/x-muted-words/).

Neither tab looks at what a post says.
