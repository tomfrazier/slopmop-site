An X List is a timeline made only of the accounts you add to it, with no recommended posts mixed in. To make one, open Lists from your profile menu, create a new List, switch on Private if you want it visible only to you, add accounts, then pin it so it sits beside For You and Following on Home. Keep it small, add accounts that post substance, and it becomes the cleanest feed X offers.

## Why X Lists make a cleaner feed

The For You tab includes recommendations, and the Following tab includes everyone you've ever followed, including accounts that drifted into bait years ago. A List is a fresh start: it shows only accounts you choose for it, and you can make as many as you need for different subjects. You can add public accounts you don't follow, which lets you read someone without adding them to your main timeline.

## Before you start

Write down 15 to 30 accounts you'd be glad to see every day. Good candidates:

- People whose posts you've quoted, saved or acted on.
- Organisations that post facts: your local council, transit operator, weather service, a journal, a regulator.
- Writers whose threads pass the check in [how to judge an X thread before you read it](/guides/x/x-thread-formula/).

Leave out accounts you follow out of loyalty or habit. They can stay in Following.

## 1. Create the List

X's help centre gives these steps ([X Help Center](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-lists)):

1. Open your profile menu. In the app, tap your profile picture; on the web, use the left-hand menu.
2. Tap or click **Lists**.
3. Tap or click the **new List** icon.
4. Give the List a name. X allows up to 25 characters, and the name can't start with a number.
5. Add a description if you like.
6. Switch on the **Private** slider if you want the List visible only to you. Lists are public by default.
7. Tap or click **Next** or **Create** to continue; the button may be labelled differently on your device.

A private List is usually the better choice for a reading feed. It's for you, and nobody else needs to see who's on it.

## 2. Add accounts

After you create the List, X prompts you to search for accounts to add. Search for each account by name or @username and add it. You can also add an account later from its profile: open the **•••** menu on the profile and look for the option to add or remove it from Lists. The wording may differ between the app and the web.

Start with fewer accounts than you think you need. A List of 20 accounts that all post well is more useful than one of 200 that mostly do.

## 3. Pin it to Home

Pinning puts the List beside your Home tabs, so you can reach it in one tap. X's help centre says that when you pin a List, it appears to the right of your For You and Following timelines ([X Help Center](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-lists)).

1. Go back to **Lists**.
2. Find your List and tap or click the **pin** icon next to it.
3. Open **Home**. The List now appears as a tab after For You and Following.

To unpin it, go back to Lists and tap the pin icon again under **Pinned**. X says unpinning doesn't delete the List. There's a limit on how many Lists you can pin, and X's help centre doesn't give one figure; TechCrunch reported that Premium subscribers using X's newer topic tabs can pin up to 10 topics or Lists in total ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/22/hands-on-with-xs-new-ai-powered-custom-feeds/)).

## 4. Make it your default

X reopens whichever Home tab you used last. If you open your List tab and leave the app there, it should be what you see next time. If X opens a different tab, swipe or click across to the List.

## X Lists that work well

| List | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Local | Council, transit, police, weather, local news desks |
| Work | People in your field who post data, methods and links |
| Sources | Primary sources: agencies, journals, companies' official accounts |
| Friends | People you actually know |

Separate Lists stop one subject drowning another. A Local List is quiet most days and very useful on the day the trains stop.

## Keep it clean

A List decays the same way a timeline does, only more slowly. Once a month:

1. Scroll the List for a few minutes.
2. For any account whose posts you skipped every time, remove it.
3. Watch for accounts that have shifted to [engagement bait](/patterns/engagement-bait/): follow-back promises, "wrong answers only", "drop yours below".
4. Add one or two accounts you've come across that consistently post things worth reading.

:::slop
Who else is grinding this Sunday? 💪 Like if you're still working 👇
:::
:::fine
Northern line: no service between Camden Town and Edgware this weekend for track work. Replacement buses every 10 minutes from stop K.
:::

If an account on your List starts posting the first kind more than the second, it's time to remove it.

## If that doesn't work

A List can still pick up noise if the accounts on it repost a lot of other people's posts. If one account keeps bringing in other people's bait through reposts, remove it from the List. For phrases you'd rather not see anywhere, add them as muted words; [how to use muted words on X](/guides/x/x-muted-words/) has the steps. For the trade-offs between your Home tabs, see [Following vs For You on X](/guides/x/x-following-vs-for-you/).
