Almost every Substack notification can be switched off from one page: **substack.com/settings**, where each section (Engagement, Messaging, Live, Connections, Notifications) lists features with separate **Email** and **Push** boxes. Untick the ones you don't want. For push notifications on your phone, the app has its own Notifications settings, and chat has a separate mute.

The one thing you can't switch off is the bell. [Substack says](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/4531662852628-What-is-the-bell-icon-on-Substack) the Activity section can't be cleared and its notifications can't be disabled. Turning off the email and push versions below still keeps them off your phone and out of your inbox.

## Before you turn off Substack notifications

- **Email and push are separate.** Unticking Email stops the emails; you'll still get phone alerts unless you also untick Push, and the other way round.
- **Do the big changes on the web.** The web Settings page has every section in one place. The app is better for push-only tweaks.
- **Decide what you want to hear about.** A sensible default for most readers: new posts from writers you pay for, replies to your own comments, direct messages. Everything else can go.

## 1. Notes: likes, replies, restacks and mentions

**On the web**

1. Go to **substack.com/settings** and scroll to **Engagement**.
2. Untick **Email** and/or **Push** next to each of these:
   - **Replies**: someone replies to your note or comment.
   - **Likes**: someone likes your note or comment.
   - **Restacks**: someone restacks your post, note or comment.
   - **Mentions**: someone mentions you in a post, note or comment.

**In the app**

Substack's Notes guide says to open your **Notifications** settings in the app, tap **Preferences**, then **Engagement**, and untick **Push** for each feature. The labels may differ slightly between iOS and Android.

**For one busy conversation**

If a single note has turned into a long thread, open the **three-dot menu** on that note and choose **Pause notifications**. That stops updates from that conversation without changing anything else.

## 2. Chat

Chats are private group spaces run by a publication. They can be noisy.

**Mute one publication's chat**

- **iOS:** tap and hold the publication and select **Mute**. Choose 1 hour, 8 hours, 24 hours, or until you unmute it.
- **Android:** tap the publication on your Chat dashboard and select **Mute**. Choose 1 hour, 8 hours, until tomorrow, or until you turn it back on.

**Turn off all chat push notifications**

- **iOS:** profile picture → **Settings** → **Notifications**, then untick every item in the **Chats** section.
- **Android:** profile picture → **Settings** → **Notifications** → **Settings**, then untick every item in the **Chat** section.

**Leave a chat altogether:** long-press the publication and select **Leave chat**.

You can also turn down how much chat appears in your Home feed, separately, with the Chat slider under **Content preferences → Content types**.

## 3. Direct messages

Notifications for new messages and message requests are on by default.

- **On the web:** in the **Messaging** section of Settings, untick **Email** and **Push** next to **Messages** and **Message Requests**.
- **In the iOS app:** profile picture → **Settings** gear → **Notifications** → **Messaging**, then untick the boxes next to **New messages** and **New message requests**. Substack says this section is called Messages on Android.

Turning off message requests while leaving messages on is a good middle setting: people you already talk to get through, strangers wait until you look.

## 4. Live video

In the **Live** section of Settings, untick **Email** and **Push** next to:

- **New live videos**: someone you follow or subscribe to goes live.
- **Live video calendar invites**: someone schedules a live video.
- **New live guest invites**: someone invites you to join their live video as a guest.

Next to **Allow live video guest invites from**, choose **Everyone**, **People I follow or subscribe to**, or **No one**. Most readers can pick No one.

## 5. New followers

If you have a profile and people follow you, you'll get an email and a bell notification. To stop the email and push versions, untick **Email** and **Push** next to **New followers** in the **Connections** section of Settings.

## 6. New posts on your phone

New-post alerts come from your **Newsletter delivery** choice. "Prefer push" or "Both" sends a phone notification for every new post from every publication you subscribe to. If that's too many, choose **Prefer email** and read on your own schedule. Our guide to [reading in the app or by email](/guides/substack/substack-app-vs-email/) has the steps and trade-offs.

**Android only:** the app's **Notifications** page has **Settings** next to **Push Notifications**, which opens your phone's settings for Substack. Under **Notification categories** you can turn individual types on or off, including Reactions, Comments, New posts and Reshare.

## What to look for after the notifications are off

Notifications pull you back to the feed, and the feed is where the bait lives. If you've turned off likes and restacks but still feel the urge to check, compare what brought you back with what you found:

:::example
Opened the app for a reply notification. Read the reply (one line, useful). Then spent 12 minutes on Home. Of the notes seen, two were worth it.
:::

If that sounds familiar, the fix is upstream: fewer writers, a cleaner feed, and a closer eye on [bait Notes](/guides/substack/substack-notes-engagement-bait/) when they appear. On a computer, [Slop Mop](/how-it-works.html) can take some of that on: it reads the Notes and posts in Substack's web app and folds the likely [engagement bait](/patterns/engagement-bait/) into a strip you can reopen. It doesn't change your notifications, and it doesn't run in the phone app.

## If that doesn't work

- **Emails still arrive after unticking.** Check whether they're newsletter posts (controlled by subscriptions and delivery, not Engagement) or recommendation digests, which have their own unsubscribe link in the email.
- **Push still arrives after unticking in Settings.** Check the app's own Notifications settings, and on Android the phone-level notification categories.
- **You can't find a section.** Settings pages change, so the menu may be labelled differently. Search the page for the feature's name, or check [Substack's help centre](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/14740062438932-How-do-I-disable-Substack-Notes-notifications) for the current wording.
