## What AI cream means

AI cream is writing made with AI help that rises to the top: it has real information in it, someone checked it, and a reader comes away knowing or able to do something they couldn't before. If [AI slop](/glossary/ai-slop/) is what you skim past, AI cream is what you save.

Things AI cream usually has:

- **Specifics from the writer's own work.** Numbers, names, dates, a trade-off they actually hit.
- **Evidence of a human pass.** Facts are right, the odd claim has a source, the filler is gone.
- **A reason to exist.** It answers a question someone has, or shows a method someone could copy.
- **Its own voice.** The AI helped with drafting or structure, but the opinions and details belong to the person.

## Where AI cream came from

Oren Etzioni and Daniel S. Weld coined it in their [October 2026 GeekWire glossary of AI-era language](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/etzioni-on-ai-the-colorful-and-cranky-language-of-the-ai-era-from-slop-to-slap/): "AI cream is our name for the rare opposite, superb writing authored with the help of AI." They hope "the cream will rise to the top and the slop will sink", and close the piece by noting that nearly every new AI-era word names a problem, and that there was still no common word for AI doing something well. AI cream is their offer.

The idea behind it is older. When Simon Willison helped popularise "slop" in May 2024, he made the point that not all AI-generated content is slop: the word fits content generated mindlessly and pushed at people who didn't ask for it. AI cream is a name for the other part.

## Examples

Two posts about the same project, both drafted with an AI model:

:::slop
We just launched our game-changing AI-powered analytics dashboard! 🚀 It will revolutionise how teams work with data. The future is here. Who's ready?
:::
:::fine
We rebuilt our reporting dashboard with an AI model writing the first draft of each SQL query. Analysts still review every query before it ships. Report requests now take 2 days instead of 6. The catch: the model kept joining on the wrong customer ID, so we added a test for that.
:::

A longer example:

:::example
A product manager asks an AI model to turn 40 pages of customer interview notes into a summary. She checks every quote against the notes, deletes three themes the model invented, and adds the one decision the team made because of it. The result is a one-page memo her team actually reads.
:::

The tool was the same in both cases. The difference is what the person did with it.

## How Slop Mop sees AI cream

Slop Mop doesn't care whether AI was involved, so AI cream gets the same treatment as good writing typed by hand: it stays in your feed, on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Facebook, Substack or Medium alike. Two of its [counter-signals](/how-it-works.html), "useful to readers" and "sounds like a person", are close to a working description of cream, and they take points off a post's slop score. The first post above, by contrast, trips [hype words](/patterns/hype-words/) and makes a promise with [no stated catch](/patterns/no-catch-promises/).
