## What AI slap means

AI slap is the act of taking what a chatbot produced and putting it in front of someone else without reading it properly first. The reply to a colleague's question, the report submitted to a client, the comment on a forum: pasted in whole, with the checking left to whoever receives it.

The person who does this is a [meat proxy](/glossary/meat-proxy/). AI slap is what they do.

The harm is a transfer of work. The sender saves a few minutes; the reader spends them, plus however long it takes to spot the wrong date, the invented source or the answer to a different question.

AI slap is a narrow charge. Using AI to draft, then reading, cutting and correcting the result, isn't AI slap. The term is for the skipped read.

## Where it came from

Oren Etzioni and Daniel S. Weld coined it in their [October 2026 GeekWire glossary of AI-era language](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/etzioni-on-ai-the-colorful-and-cranky-language-of-the-ai-era-from-slop-to-slap/), which takes its headline from it: "We call the action of sending AI-generated text blindly an AI slap. It stings." They pair it with meat proxy, which they quote from [SFisms](https://sfisms.org/meat-proxy) as "a person who forwards AI-generated text, code, or other output without reading, understanding, or validating it."

Other people have named the same habit. "Sloppypasta", a blend of slop and copypasta, is used for verbatim chatbot output pasted at someone without being read, refined or requested. The "Stop Sloppypasta" page that popularised it argues that pasting asks the recipient to do work the sender didn't bother to do. At work, the result often gets called [workslop](/glossary/workslop/).

## Examples

A reply in a team chat to "Does our contract with Acme auto-renew?":

:::slop
Certainly! Here's a comprehensive overview of contract auto-renewal. Auto-renewal clauses are common in many commercial agreements. It's important to review the specific terms of your contract. Furthermore, you may wish to consult your legal team. I hope this helps!
:::
:::fine
Yes, clause 14.2: it renews for 12 months unless we give 60 days' notice. The next notice deadline is 2 February.
:::

Leftover chat-assistant phrases are the giveaway in the first one. Nobody read it closely enough to delete them.

:::example
A job applicant submits a cover letter that still contains "[Insert company name]" in the second paragraph.
:::

## How Slop Mop spots an AI slap

Slop Mop can't tell whether someone read their own post, and it doesn't try to tell whether AI wrote it. What it can see is the result: AI slap tends to leave [stiff phrasing](/patterns/stiff-phrasing/), filler and claims with nothing behind them, and those are among the tells it scores in your feeds on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Facebook, Substack and Medium. A lot of AI slap arrives by email, and [Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook](/for/email/), part of Slop Mop XL, checks what the sender wrote there too.
