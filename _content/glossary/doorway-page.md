## What it means

A doorway page is built for a search engine, not for the person who lands on it. Its job is to rank for one query, catch the click and pass the visitor on to the page the site actually wanted them to see. The doorway itself says little or nothing of use.

The classic version is a set of near-identical pages, one for each town or product variant, where only a word or two changes. "Emergency plumber in Leeds", "Emergency plumber in Bradford", "Emergency plumber in Wakefield": same text, same phone number, same link to the booking page, with the city name swapped in.

There's nothing wrong with location pages as such. A business with real branches, staff or service areas can describe each one. The line is crossed when the pages exist only to multiply search entries and describe nothing real.

## Where it came from

Doorway pages are one of the older black-hat SEO tricks, in use since the early days of web search, which is why the term has no clear coiner or date. Early versions were sometimes stuffed with keywords and hidden from human visitors by an instant redirect.

Google's spam policies now call the practice doorway abuse. They define it as sites or pages "created to rank for specific, similar search queries" that "lead users to intermediate pages that aren't as useful as the final destination." The examples Google lists include multiple domains or pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page, and substantially similar pages that are "closer to search results than a clearly defined, browseable hierarchy."

## Examples

Two pages for a removals company. The first is one of 40 copies; the second is one of 3 real branches.

:::slop
Looking for reliable removals in Harrogate? Our Harrogate removals team offers the best removals Harrogate has to offer. Whether you're moving house in Harrogate or relocating your Harrogate office, we deliver a stress-free service. Call now for a free Harrogate removals quote!
:::
:::fine
Our Harrogate depot is on Hookstone Road and runs 2 crews. Most local moves take one day; we can't do Saturday moves in the town centre before 8am because of loading restrictions. Typical cost for a 3-bedroom house within the district: £650 to £900.
:::

The first page would read the same with any town name. The second contains facts that only apply to one place.
