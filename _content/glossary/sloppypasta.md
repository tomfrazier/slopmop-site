## What sloppypasta means

Sloppypasta is what lands in your inbox, your team chat or a comment thread when someone asks a chatbot and pastes the answer straight at you. It's long, oddly formal, a little off-topic, and still says "I hope this helps!" at the bottom. The sender spent ten seconds. You spend five minutes working out which parts are true.

The word is a blend of slop and [copypasta](/glossary/copypasta/), the internet's habit of pasting the same block of text everywhere. The difference is that a copypasta is pasted on purpose, usually as a joke. Sloppypasta is pasted because reading it felt like too much effort.

On this site it has two close relatives. Sending sloppypasta is an [AI slap](/glossary/ai-slap/), and the person who sends it is a [meat proxy](/glossary/meat-proxy/). At work the whole thing often goes by [workslop](/glossary/workslop/).

## Where the word sloppypasta came from

The word spread in March 2026 with [Stop Sloppypasta](https://stopsloppypasta.ai/en/), a one-page site in the spirit of nohello.net and dontasktoask.com: a page you can link to instead of explaining yourself for the hundredth time. It defines sloppypasta as "verbatim LLM output copy-pasted at someone, unread, unrefined, and unrequested", and argues that pasting it hands the recipient work the sender didn't do. The site's code is [open source on GitHub](https://github.com/ahgraber/stopsloppypasta). By mid-March it was being passed around blogs, including a link post on [Adactio](https://adactio.com/links/tags/sloppypasta).

The page's advice fits in one breath: read it first, check the facts, cut it down, say you used AI, share it only when asked, and send a link instead of a wall of text.

## Examples

A colleague asks in the team chat: "Is the office open on Monday?"

:::slop
Great question! Office opening hours can vary depending on a number of factors, including public holidays, company policy and local regulations. It's important to check your organisation's official communications for the most accurate information. Additionally, you may wish to contact your HR department. I hope this helps! 😊
:::
:::fine
Yes, normal hours. The holiday closure is the Monday after, the 12th.
:::

The tell is that the first reply could have been sent to anyone in any company on Earth. Nobody who knew the answer wrote it, and nobody who sent it read it.

## How Slop Mop sees sloppypasta

Slop Mop can't tell whether someone read what they posted, and it doesn't try to tell whether AI wrote it. It judges the writing. Sloppypasta tends to arrive full of [stiff phrasing](/patterns/stiff-phrasing/), filler and generic warmth with no detail from anyone's actual life, which scores low on its "sounds like a person" check. That's true in your feeds on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Facebook, Substack and Medium, and in email: [Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook](/for/email/), part of Slop Mop XL, checks what the sender wrote there too.
