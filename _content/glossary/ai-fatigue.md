## What AI fatigue means

AI fatigue is the tired, slightly glazed feeling that comes from too much AI at once. Route Fifty, a trade publication for state and local government, defined it as "the exhaustion or the sense of being overwhelmed by the rapid proliferation and constant engagement with artificial intelligence technologies". The phrase stretches to cover several things:

- **News fatigue.** Another model launch, another "everything just changed" thread, every week.
- **Tool fatigue.** A new assistant in every app you use, each with its own button, settings and pop-up.
- **Content fatigue.** Feeds and inboxes full of [AI slop](/glossary/ai-slop/) you have to wade through.
- **Pressure fatigue.** The sense that you're falling behind if you don't learn all of it, now.

It's an everyday phrase, in the way "Zoom fatigue" was. It isn't a clinical condition, though it can sit alongside ordinary work stress and burnout. If tiredness or low mood is lasting, that's worth talking to a doctor about.

## Where it came from

The phrase is plain description and has no known coiner. "Fatigue" has long been attached to whatever is overwhelming people at the time, from "compassion fatigue" to "Zoom fatigue". "AI fatigue" followed as generative AI tools spread through workplaces. Oren Etzioni and Daniel S. Weld included it in their [October 2026 GeekWire glossary of AI-era language](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/etzioni-on-ai-the-colorful-and-cranky-language-of-the-ai-era-from-slop-to-slap/), noting that it has drifted through the trade press since 2023 with no author attached, and quoting the [Route Fifty definition](https://www.route-fifty.com/emerging-tech/2024/09/ai-fatigue/399671/).

Surveys give a rough sense of the feeling, though they measure different things. In a May 2025 survey of UK workers, Henley Business School found 56% felt optimistic about AI and 61% felt overwhelmed by how fast it was moving. Its 2026 follow-up, with 2,900 workers, found the overwhelmed figure still at 61%.

## Examples

:::slop
🚨 AI just changed EVERYTHING (again). 10 new tools you MUST learn this week or get left behind. Bookmark this. 🧵
:::
:::fine
We tried four AI note-takers this quarter. We kept one, because it's the only one that exports to our wiki. The other three cost us about two hours each to set up and remove.
:::

The first kind of post causes AI fatigue. The second helps cure it: it narrows the choice, says what it cost, and lets you stop looking.

:::example
"I've got AI fatigue. I'm ignoring every launch until something I use every day actually gets better."
:::
