## What it means

"Feel the AGI" means having a moment where artificial general intelligence, AI that can do most of what people can, suddenly seems real and close. Someone watches a model solve a problem they thought it couldn't, and they feel the AGI.

Inside parts of the AI industry it has been used sincerely, as a way of saying "take this seriously, it's coming". Outside, it's mostly used with a smirk, for the near-spiritual excitement that can surround AI launches: the keynote applause, the posts announcing that everything has changed, the conviction that this demo is the one.

The phrase is a metaphor. Saying someone "feels the AGI" is a joke about tone and mood, and it doesn't tell you what any particular person actually believes.

## Where it came from

The phrase entered wide circulation in November 2023, during the boardroom crisis in which OpenAI briefly removed Sam Altman as chief executive. Reporting by Karen Hao and Charlie Warzel in The Atlantic, drawing on interviews with current and former employees, described a company culture in which the phrase was a rallying cry. Futurism, summarising that reporting, wrote that employees had reportedly chanted "Feel the AGI! Feel the AGI!", led by Ilya Sutskever, then OpenAI's chief scientist. These are accounts from people who worked there, relayed by journalists, and they don't amount to a statement of Sutskever's own beliefs.

From there it spread as a meme across the tech industry, sometimes as an in-joke among enthusiasts and often as a jab at them.

## Examples

Two posts about the same model release, written our own way:

:::slop
I just tried the new model and I'm genuinely shaking. This changes EVERYTHING. We are witnessing the dawn of a new era for humanity. Feel the AGI. 🚀
:::
:::fine
I gave the new model our 40 hardest support tickets. It solved 29, got 6 confidently wrong and refused 5. Better than last month's 18, still not something I'd let reply to customers alone.
:::

And the ironic use, as it tends to appear:

:::example
"The product manager put 'feel the AGI' on a slide about a chatbot that books meeting rooms. Nobody in the room felt it."
:::

## How Slop Mop sees it

Slop Mop doesn't take a view on whether AGI is close. It does read the writing: launch posts full of [hype words](/patterns/hype-words/) and [flowery prose](/patterns/flowery-prose/) about dawns and new eras score as slop on LinkedIn, and a post with test results and failure rates scores as useful to readers, however excited its author is.
