## What chatbait means

Chatbait is [clickbait](/glossary/clickbait/) that learned to talk. You ask a chatbot a question, it answers, and then it dangles something: "Want me to turn this into a checklist?", "Shall I draft the email for you?", "I can also make a version for your boss!" Clickbait gets you to open a link. Chatbait gets you to send one more message, and then another.

A single offer can be genuinely helpful. The word is for the habit: replies that always end on a hook, offers of things you never asked for, and the faint feeling that the conversation is designed to have no natural end. Critics add a second worry. The longer you chat, the more you tend to tell the bot about yourself.

## Where the word chatbait came from

The word took off with Lila Shroff's September 2025 piece in The Atlantic, [Chatbait Is Taking Over the Internet](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2025/09/chatbait-ai-chatgpt-engagement/684300/). We haven't found an earlier use. [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/chatbait-is-the-new-clickbait-heres-how-chatbots-are-keeping-you-hooked) picked it up the same month, describing chatbait as the way AI systems nudge you into longer conversations, and pointing out what a company gets from that: more time on the app, better retention numbers and more conversation data.

It belongs to a family. A chatbot that flatters you so you stay is showing [AI sycophancy](/glossary/ai-sycophancy/). One that makes you angry so you stay would be [rage bait](/glossary/rage-bait/). Chatbait is the polite, eager cousin: it just really, really wants to help some more.

## Examples

You ask: "What's the capital of Australia?"

:::slop
Great question! The capital of Australia is Canberra. 🇦🇺 Would you like me to plan a 7-day Canberra itinerary, explain why it isn't Sydney, or quiz you on every capital in Oceania? Just say the word!
:::
:::fine
Canberra.
:::

The same trick shows up in feed posts written by people, where it's an older tell with a different name:

:::slop
I've put together my full 47-step framework for writing better emails. Want it? Comment "EMAIL" and I'll send it over. 👇
:::
