## What it means

Agentsomnia is a joke word for losing sleep over AI agents. You leave an agent working overnight on a long task and then lie awake wondering whether it's stuck, burning through your usage limit, or quietly rewriting the wrong files. It's the night-time cousin of [Agent FOMO](/glossary/agent-fomo/).

The word plays on insomnia, but it is not a medical term and not a sleep disorder. If you're regularly struggling to sleep, for any reason, that's worth raising with a doctor.

## Where it came from

Agentsomnia is a Slop Mop house coinage; we haven't found it in established use. Developers have joked about the same thing in other words. "Agentphasic sleep", a play on polyphasic sleep, has been used on X and in newsletters for people who break up their night to keep agents running or to restart them when they hit a limit.

The joke depends on agents that can work for hours without supervision. It lands because the worry has a practical basis: an agent left overnight can stall on a question in the first ten minutes, or keep going confidently in the wrong direction until morning, and both waste the night.

The usual cures are dull and effective. Give the agent a clear stopping point, a limit on what it may spend or change, and permission to stop and leave a note when it's unsure. Then check it in the morning, at a time you chose.

## Examples

:::example
At 2am a founder wakes up, checks her laptop and finds the overnight agent stopped at 11:15pm to ask whether it could install a package. She approves it and goes back to bed. She does it again at 4am.
:::

:::example
A developer starts three agents before bed, props his phone against the lamp with the status page open, and falls asleep refreshing it.
:::

:::example
Used in a sentence: "Sorry I'm slow this morning. Agentsomnia. The migration ran fine, I just didn't trust it to."
:::
