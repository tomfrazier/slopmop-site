## What a slopper is

A slopper is someone who hands every small decision to an AI chatbot: what to eat, what to say, how to answer a friend's message. The word is a jokey insult, built on [AI slop](/glossary/ai-slop/), and it targets the habit more than the technology. Using a chatbot to check a contract clause wouldn't earn the label. Asking it what to order on a first date might.

Where a [meat proxy](/glossary/meat-proxy/) passes AI output on to other people without reading it, a slopper's reliance is mostly personal. The worry behind the joke is that thinking for yourself is a skill, and a slopper has stopped practising it.

## Where slopper came from

The term spread on TikTok in mid-2025. [Futurism reported](https://futurism.com/sloppers-ask-ai-everything) on 29 July 2025 that a TikTok user had posted a viral video saying a friend had coined "sloppers" for people using ChatGPT to do everything for them. The media critic Rusty Foster picked it up in his newsletter Today in Tabs, pointing to another viral video in which a man described a first date who asked ChatGPT what to order from the menu. "There was no second date," he said.

Futurism noted that the term wasn't settled; commenters offered alternatives, including "botlicker" and "second hand thinker". It compared the word to "glassholes", the nickname given to early wearers of Google Glass, and said the coinage highlighted growing anti-AI resentment. Foster ended his piece with a warning: "Every sign points to a full-blown slopper pandemic ahead."

## Examples

:::example
A group chat is planning a Saturday hike. One friend replies with a 6-point itinerary covering "optimal trail selection" and "hydration considerations" for a walk the group has done four times.
:::

:::example
Someone at dinner pauses the conversation to ask their phone whether they should get the risotto.
:::

Neither is harmful. The label is about the reflex: reaching for the chatbot before trying to decide.

Slopper describes a personal habit, which is outside what Slop Mop judges.
