## What chatfishing means

Chatfishing is catfishing's AI-era cousin. A catfish lies about who they are, usually with someone else's photos. A chatfish is often exactly who their photos say, but their messages are written, polished or entirely run by a chatbot. The match falls for the banter, then meets someone who talks nothing like it.

It covers a wide range, and most of it is mild:

- asking a chatbot for an opening line
- pasting in a stalled conversation and asking what to say next
- having AI rewrite a message so it sounds warmer or funnier
- at the far end, letting a tool carry the whole conversation

Using a writing aid isn't automatically deception, any more than asking a friend to check a text before you send it. The word usually stings when the gap gets big: when the person on the other side thinks they're getting to know you and is mostly getting to know a model.

## Where chatfishing came from

The word is a play on "catfishing" and spread in dating coverage in 2025. A Guardian feature in October 2025 used it as its headline term, describing daters who had been "Chatfished" and some who called themselves "Chatfishers". It reported that most say they would never let AI do all their talking, while some outsource entire conversations. We haven't found a reliable record of who used the word first. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionaryblog.cambridge.org/2026/01/12/new-words-12-january-2026/) listed it among its new words in January 2026, and Oren Etzioni and Daniel S. Weld included it in their [October 2026 GeekWire glossary of AI-era language](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/etzioni-on-ai-the-colorful-and-cranky-language-of-the-ai-era-from-slop-to-slap/), observing that on dating apps "the con used to be a fake photo and is now a rented personality." At the far end, a chatbot running the whole conversation starts to look like Daniel Dennett's [counterfeit people](/glossary/counterfeit-people/).

## Examples

Two replies to the same dating-app message: "What did you get up to this weekend?"

:::slop
Ah, weekends are such a beautiful tapestry of rest and adventure! ✨ I believe life is all about savouring the little moments. What fills your cup?
:::
:::fine
Fixed my bike, then rode it straight into a puddle. Also finally tried the dumpling place on Mill Road. Have you been? The chilli oil is worth the queue.
:::

The first could have been sent by anyone to anyone. The second is so specific that a match will know who to expect on the date.

A disclosed use, which most people wouldn't call chatfishing:

:::example
"Full disclosure, I asked ChatGPT how to say this without sounding weird, and it was useless, so here's my version: I'd like to see you again."
:::
