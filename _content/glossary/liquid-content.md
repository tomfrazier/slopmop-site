## What liquid content means

Liquid content is the idea that a piece of writing no longer has to stay in one shape. With generative AI, the same article can be poured into a 30-second summary, a podcast, a quiz, a LinkedIn carousel or a version for a ten-year-old, depending on who's reading and how much time they have. The article works more like a tap you pour from.

For readers that can be handy. You get the report as a two-minute listen on the bus. For publishers it's an open question with a scary edge: if the reader only ever meets the reshaped version, what exactly are they paying for?

## Where the term liquid content came from

The phrase circulates among people who build media and AI products. In March 2025 Farsight, the magazine of the Copenhagen Institute for Futures Studies, ran [The Anything-to-Everything Future Is Here](https://farsight.cifs.dk/the-anything-to-everything-future-is-here/), which says AI "is enabling a world of 'liquid content'", where stories and images move freely between formats. The piece is built around an interview with Google DeepMind's Matthieu Lorrain and asks whether the notion of a "finished" piece will dissolve. Lorrain's answer, as Farsight reports it, is that audiences still see a finished state.

The tools already exist in small ways. Text-to-speech reads articles aloud, chatbots summarise them, and Google's NotebookLM turns your documents into a two-host audio show, which is where the [fakecast](/glossary/fakecast/) comes from. We haven't found who used "liquid content" first.

## The catch with liquid content

Most pours lose something. Nuance, caveats, the reporter's careful "probably", the one sentence that changes the meaning: these are the first things to go when a machine squeezes 3,000 words into a quote card. The result often reads like [flowery prose](/patterns/flowery-prose/) with the facts boiled off. And when anyone can reshape anything instantly, you get more of everything, which feeds [content shock](/glossary/content-shock/).

There's an older cousin too. [Article spinning](/glossary/article-spinning/) reworded one article into many to game search engines. Liquid content can be the honest version of that, or the same trick with better tools.

## Examples

The same council report, poured two ways:

:::slop
🚨 The council just dropped its waste report and it's WILD. Recycling is the hero we didn't know we needed. Swipe to discover 7 mind-blowing takeaways ➡️
:::
:::fine
Summary of the council's 2026 waste report: recycling rose, general waste fell, and food-waste collection starts in April. Figures and caveats are in section 3 of the full report.
:::
