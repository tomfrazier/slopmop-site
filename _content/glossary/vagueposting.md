## What vagueposting means

Vagueposting is posting something cryptic without the context needed to understand it. "Some people really show you who they are." "Well, that's that, then." "Big news coming 👀." The post hints that something happened and leaves out what, who and why, so readers reply to ask.

Sometimes that's the aim: a vaguepost is a reliable way to get comments and concern, which makes it a cousin of [engagement bait](/patterns/engagement-bait/). Sometimes it's just poor communication, someone thinking aloud in public and forgetting that nobody else was in the room. And sometimes it's aimed at one person who will know it's about them, while everyone else can only guess.

## Where vagueposting came from

"Vaguebooking" was coined for deliberately vague Facebook statuses that prompted friends to follow up. "Vagueposting" is the platform-neutral form; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vagueposting) dates it to 2011 and says its use took off in the 2020s on Twitter and TikTok.

On LinkedIn the genre has its own accent: the teaser announcement ("Can't say much yet, but something big is coming"), the cryptic career update ("After a lot of reflection, I've made a decision") and the unnamed grievance ("Interesting to see who reaches out when you're down").

## Examples

:::slop
Some days you learn exactly who's in your corner. Grateful for the few. Noted the rest. 🙏
:::
:::fine
Our launch has moved from 21 October to 4 November. A supplier pulled out last week and we've signed a new one. Customers on the waiting list will hear from us by Friday.
:::

The first invites a dozen "Are you OK?" replies and answers none of them. The second could have been vague ("Some exciting changes ahead!") and instead says what changed, why and what happens next.

:::slop
Big things coming. Can't say more yet. Watch this space 👀
:::
:::fine
We're opening a second clinic in Bristol in January. Bookings open on 1 December; I'll post the address when the lease is signed.
:::

## How Slop Mop sees it

A vaguepost gives a reader nothing to learn or act on, so it scores poorly on Slop Mop's "useful to readers" counter-signal, and teaser lines like "watch this space" read as a [scroll-stopper](/patterns/scroll-stopper/). A short, cryptic personal post may still sound like a person and isn't always folded.
