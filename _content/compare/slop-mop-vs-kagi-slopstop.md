## What each one does

Kagi SlopStop and Slop Mop share most of a name and a dislike of slop. They work in different places: one in search results, the other in a social feed.

**SlopStop** is a feature of Kagi, a paid, ad-free search engine. Kagi launched it on 12 November 2025 as a community-driven way to deal with mass-produced AI content in web, image and video results. Any Kagi user can click the shield icon next to a result and report it as AI-generated; Kagi checks the report against its own signals. According to Kagi's documentation, a page that's AI-generated on an otherwise mixed site is flagged but not downranked. A domain that's mostly AI-generated (typically more than 80% of its pages) is flagged as AI slop and downranked, with subdomains judged separately. AI-generated images and videos are labelled and downranked, and you can filter them out entirely.

**Slop Mop** is a browser extension that reads the posts in your LinkedIn feed before you reach them. An AI model designed for calibrated decisions answers twelve questions about each post: nine tells of slop and three counter-signals (sounds like a person, useful to readers, reader response). Likely slop folds into a one-line strip you can reopen, or, in Highlight mode, is marked and left visible.

## Where they differ

**Where they work.** SlopStop covers Kagi's search results. Slop Mop covers your LinkedIn feed today, with X, Reddit, Facebook, Substack, Medium, websites and Gmail and Outlook coming soon. If you're tired of recipe sites and how-to pages padded out by AI, SlopStop is aimed at you. If you're tired of hooks and engagement bait between your colleagues' posts, Slop Mop is.

**What counts as slop.** Kagi defines AI slop as deceptive or low-value AI-generated content made to manipulate ranking or attention, and it says it isn't against AI tools in general. Its focus is still on AI content: a site gets downranked for being mostly AI-generated. Slop Mop judges the writing whoever produced it, so a human-written post full of bait can be folded and a useful AI-assisted one can stay.

**Scale of judgement.** SlopStop mostly acts on whole domains and channels, which suits search: one content farm can produce thousands of pages, and downranking the domain deals with all of them at once. Slop Mop scores each post on its own, which suits a feed where the same person might write one useful post and one empty one in the same week.

**People or model.** SlopStop starts from community reports reviewed by Kagi, and Kagi has said it plans to add more automation over time. Slop Mop scores every post automatically as you scroll; your own votes override it in your feed but don't change anyone else's.

**What happens to slop.** SlopStop downranks and labels, so slop moves down the page rather than disappearing. Slop Mop folds a post into a strip or, in Highlight mode, only marks it. Neither deletes anything, and Slop Mop never reports, mutes or blocks.

**Price and account.** SlopStop comes with Kagi Search. As of October 2026 Kagi offers a free trial of 100 searches, then Starter at US$5 a month, Professional at US$10 and Ultimate at US$25. Slop Mop is free for one feed with 250 checks a day, and Slop Mop XL (coming soon) will be US$5.99 a month or US$59.99 a year.

## When to use Kagi SlopStop

- AI content farms and generated images are making your searches worse.
- You want an ad-free search engine anyway and are happy to pay for one.
- You like the idea of reporting slop and helping other users avoid it.
- You want AI-generated images and videos filtered out of results.

## When to use Slop Mop

- Your problem is the LinkedIn feed, not search.
- You want each post judged on its own, with the reasons shown.
- You want human-written filler caught as well as AI-generated filler.
- You want a free option that doesn't need a new account with a search engine.

## Using both

These two don't overlap at all today, so running both is easy: Kagi for searching, Slop Mop for reading LinkedIn. When Slop Mop's support for websites arrives (it's coming soon, not live), the two will meet more often, but SlopStop will still be working on which results you see and Slop Mop on what you read once you're there.
