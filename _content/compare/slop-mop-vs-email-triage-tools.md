Email triage tools and Slop Mop both aim to give you back time spent on mail that wasn't worth it. They come at it from opposite ends. Triage tools decide which senders deserve your attention. Slop Mop decides whether a particular piece of writing does. And there's a timing difference that matters more than either: triage tools work on email today, and Slop Mop's email support is still coming soon.

## What each one does

**Newsletter unsubscribe and email triage tools** connect to your mailbox and act on senders and categories. Examples include [SaneBox](/compare/slop-mop-vs-sanebox/), which sorts incoming mail by importance, and [Unroll.Me](/compare/slop-mop-vs-unroll-me/), which lists your subscriptions so you can unsubscribe in bulk or roll them into a daily digest. They work with Gmail, Outlook and other providers. Pricing ranges from free to paid subscription, and the business models differ: [Unroll.Me's data page](https://unroll.me/your-data) says it's owned by NielsenIQ and uses data from your commercial emails for market research and advertising products, while [SaneBox](https://www.sanebox.com/) is a paid service with a free trial.

**Slop Mop** is a browser extension that reads content before you reach it and judges the writing. An AI model designed for calibrated decisions rates nine tells of slop, such as [hype words](/patterns/hype-words/) and [stiff phrasing](/patterns/stiff-phrasing/), and three counter-signals, then gives a score out of 100. As of October 2026 it works on LinkedIn. Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook is coming soon, as part of Slop Mop XL.

## Where they differ

**Availability.** This comes first because it decides the question for most people today. Triage tools work on your inbox now. Slop Mop doesn't, yet. If your inbox is the problem this week, a triage tool is the one that can help.

**Senders versus writing.** Triage tools judge the sender: is this someone you reply to, a newsletter you read, a shop you bought from once? That works well for clearing out whole categories. It can't help with the newsletter you subscribed to on purpose that has slipped into filler, or the internal update from a colleague that says nothing in 600 words (what's sometimes called [workslop](/glossary/workslop/)). Judging each message on its writing is what Slop Mop is built to do.

:::slop
Hi team! Just circling back to touch base on our Q3 learnings journey. Moving forward, let's continue to align on synergies and keep the momentum going! 🚀
:::
:::fine
Q3: we shipped the export fix and missed the SSO deadline by two weeks. SSO is now due 14 November; Priya owns it. No action needed from you.
:::

Both come from the same sender, so a sender-based tool treats them the same.

**What happens to the mail.** Triage tools move, roll up, block or unsubscribe. That's their strength: they clear things out. Slop Mop never deletes, blocks, reports or unsubscribes anything. It folds an item in place, or marks it, and one click opens it.

**Reasons and control.** Slop Mop shows the score and strongest signs for each item and lets you vote to overrule it. Triage tools generally let you train them by moving mail between folders or marking senders.

**Privacy.** Data handling varies a lot between triage tools, from paid services to free ones funded by data use, so read each tool's terms before connecting your mailbox. Slop Mop never sends your name or the author's. It sends the text to be scored and keeps a one-way hash of the text and the scores; the one exception is an item you vote against, whose text is kept so the miss can be reviewed.

## When to use an email triage tool

- Newsletters, notifications and promotions are swamping your inbox right now.
- You want to unsubscribe from many senders at once.
- You'd like low-priority mail moved out of the way and summarised in a digest.

## When to use Slop Mop

- Today: for your LinkedIn feed, where it's live, free for one feed with 250 checks a day.
- Later: when Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook arrives with Slop Mop XL (US$5.99 a month or US$59.99 a year), for the mail you've chosen to keep but don't always want to read in full.

## Using both

Once Slop Mop's email support is available, the two would fit together: a triage tool clears out senders you don't want, and Slop Mop judges the writing of what's left. Neither replaces your [spam filter](/compare/slop-mop-vs-spam-filters/), which handles junk and scams. If you'd rather move newsletters out of email entirely, [feed readers](/compare/slop-mop-vs-rss-readers/) are another option.
