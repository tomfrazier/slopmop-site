This comparison comes with a big caveat up front. SaneBox works on email today. Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook is coming soon, but it isn't live: as of October 2026, Slop Mop works only on LinkedIn. If your inbox is the problem right now, SaneBox is the one of these two that can help this week.

## What each one does

**SaneBox** is an email assistant that works with any email provider and client, with nothing to install ([SaneBox](https://www.sanebox.com/)). It moves unimportant mail out of your inbox into separate folders, and adds a set of tools around that: a Daily Digest summarising unimportant mail you haven't opened, BlackHole for senders you never want to hear from again, one-click unsubscribe, reminders when someone hasn't replied, Snooze for mail that should come back later, and SaneNews, which rounds up newsletters into one folder ([SaneBox tour](https://www.sanebox.com/tour)). SaneBox says it leaves your email on your mail server and analyses only the headers to decide what's important ([SaneBox security](https://www.sanebox.com/security)). It's a paid service after a free trial. As of October 2026, a third-party pricing tracker lists three plans, from about US$7 a month for one account to about US$36 a month for four ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/sanebox/pricing)); check SaneBox's own pricing page before you buy.

**Slop Mop** is a Chrome and Chromium extension that reads each LinkedIn post as you scroll. An AI model designed for calibrated decisions answers twelve questions about the writing: nine tells of slop, such as [hype words](/patterns/hype-words/) and [stiff phrasing](/patterns/stiff-phrasing/), and three counter-signals, such as whether the post is useful to readers. Each post gets a score out of 100. Hide mode folds likely slop into a one-line strip you can reopen; Highlight mode marks it and hides nothing. Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook is planned for the same kind of filler in email, but it's coming soon, not live. X, Reddit, Facebook, Substack, Medium and websites are on the same coming-soon list.

## Where they differ

**Availability.** SaneBox handles email now, on any account and any device, including your phone. Slop Mop handles LinkedIn now, in a desktop Chromium browser. For email, there's nothing to compare yet.

**What they judge.** SaneBox asks whether an email matters to you, based on its headers and on how you train it. Slop Mop asks whether a piece of writing is worth reading: whether it's built on hooks, bait and padding, and whether it says anything useful. The two questions overlap less than you'd think. An important email from your manager can be badly written, and a well-written newsletter can be one you never open. SaneBox's question is the more useful one for inbox triage, where who sent it often matters most.

**What happens to the item.** SaneBox moves mail between folders on your mail server, and BlackHole sends a sender's mail away for good. Slop Mop changes only what you see in your browser. It never deletes, reports, mutes or blocks anything, and a folded post is one click from being read.

**How you correct it.** In SaneBox you refile mail, and the Daily Digest has bulk actions that train it where to file messages ([SaneBox tour](https://www.sanebox.com/tour)). In Slop Mop you click the mop icon to see the score and the reasons, then vote Not sure, No, Maybe or Probably; your vote overrides the model in your own feed.

**Privacy.** SaneBox needs access to your mailbox to do its job, and says it analyses headers and doesn't store email bodies that optional features retrieve ([SaneBox security](https://www.sanebox.com/security)). Slop Mop sends post text (never your name or the author's) to its scoring service; the server keeps a one-way hash of the text and the scores, except for posts where you vote against the score.

**Price.** SaneBox has no permanent free plan, per the same tracker. Slop Mop is free for one feed with 250 checks a day. Slop Mop XL, coming soon, will be US$5.99 a month or US$59.99 a year and will add every feed, the sites you read, and Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook.

## When to use SaneBox

- You spend too long each day deciding which email to open first.
- You want low-priority mail gathered into one digest instead of scattered through your inbox.
- You want follow-up reminders, Snooze and an easy way to banish a sender.
- You need it to work on your phone and in any mail app, today.

## When to use Slop Mop

- Your time goes on the LinkedIn feed, and the problem is the quality of the posts.
- You want each post judged on its writing, with reasons you can see and a vote that overrules the model.
- You'd like to try something free before paying for anything.
- For email, Slop Mop is a future option, not a current one. If you want it there, watch for its Gmail and Outlook support.

## Using both

Today they cover different places, so you can run both without overlap: SaneBox on your inbox, Slop Mop on LinkedIn. Once Slop Mop's email support arrives, the two would answer different questions about the same inbox: SaneBox deciding what matters to you, Slop Mop flagging the filler inside what's left.
