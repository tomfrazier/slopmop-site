Both tools share a word and a worry: there's a lot of low-effort writing online and it's getting harder to find the good stuff. They answer it in opposite ways. Slop Evader steps back to an older web. Slop Mop stays in today's feed and sorts it.

## What each one does

**Slop Evader** is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox made by the artist Tega Brain, published in late October 2025 ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/01/pregpt_slop_evader_browser/)). It filters your Google searches so that results only include pages published before 30 November 2022, the day ChatGPT was first released to the public ([Tega Brain](https://tegabrain.com/Slop-Evader)). It does this by adding Google's own date-range filter to your query. It also has shortcuts for searching Reddit, Quora, Stack Exchange, Mumsnet, Pinterest and YouTube the same way.

Brain describes the project as "a small act of digital refusal", and on its GitHub page calls the extension "just a convenience": you could get the same results by typing Google's date operators yourself ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/01/pregpt_slop_evader_browser/)). It's free.

**Slop Mop** is a Chrome and Chromium extension that reads each LinkedIn post as you scroll and asks twelve questions about the writing: nine tells of slop, such as [engagement bait](/patterns/engagement-bait/) and [hype words](/patterns/hype-words/), and three counter-signals, such as whether the post is useful to readers. Each post gets a score out of 100. In Hide mode, likely slop folds into a one-line strip you can reopen; in Highlight mode, it stays visible and gets marked. X, Reddit, Facebook, Substack, Medium, websites and email are coming soon, but LinkedIn is the only network it works on today.

## Where they differ

**What counts as slop.** Slop Evader uses a date as its test. Anything after November 2022 is out, including careful, useful writing published last week. Anything before is in, including the keyword-stuffed pages and content farms that existed long before ChatGPT. Slop Mop reads the post itself, so a good post written yesterday survives and an empty one from any year can be flagged. It judges the writing, not who or what wrote it, so a useful post drafted with AI help is treated like any other useful post.

**Where you use it.** Slop Evader works on search, the moment you go looking for something. Slop Mop works in a feed, where things come to you. If your problem is search results full of AI-written how-to pages, Slop Evader goes straight at it, and Slop Mop doesn't touch search at all.

**Simplicity.** Slop Evader is about as simple as a tool can be: one rule, no account and nothing to tune. Slop Mop sends post text (never your name or the author's) to its scoring service and gives you settings to manage: two modes, three sensitivity levels and a vote. If you want something with no moving parts, Slop Evader wins easily.

**Explaining itself.** Slop Evader doesn't need to explain a verdict, because it never gives one about a page. Slop Mop shows the score, the strongest signs and a chart of all nine tells on any post you click, and lets you vote to overrule it in your own feed.

**Cost and limits.** Slop Evader is free. Slop Mop is free for one feed with 250 checks a day; Slop Mop XL, coming soon, will be US$5.99 a month or US$59.99 a year for every feed and a higher limit.

## When to use Slop Evader

- You're researching something that doesn't change much (a recipe, a DIY repair, an old forum answer) and you'd rather read pages written before generative AI took off.
- You want to search Reddit, Stack Exchange or Mumsnet without wading through recent AI-written threads.
- You like the idea of the extension as a statement, and the trade-off of losing new material doesn't bother you for this search.
- You use Firefox. Slop Mop doesn't run there yet.

Slop Evader is a poor fit when you need anything current: news, prices, new software, recent research or anyone's latest work.

## When to use Slop Mop

- Your time goes on LinkedIn and the problem is the feed: hooks, bait, [empty praise](/patterns/empty-praise/) and filler between the posts worth reading.
- You want new posts kept and judged on their merits, not discarded for being new.
- You want to see why something was folded and be able to disagree.

Slop Mop is a poor fit if your main problem is search results, or if you want a tool that sends nothing anywhere.

## Using both

They cover different moments, so running both causes no conflict: Slop Evader when you search, Slop Mop when you scroll LinkedIn.
