## What DuckDuckGo's AI image filter and Slop Mop do

DuckDuckGo's "Hide AI-Generated Images" setting and Slop Mop both deal with slop, but they don't touch the same material. One works on pictures in search results; the other on written posts in a social feed.

**DuckDuckGo's filter** is a search setting [added in July 2025](https://www.techradar.com/computing/artificial-intelligence/sick-of-seeing-ai-images-everywhere-online-duckduckgo-will-let-you-filter-out-the-slop-from-your-search-results). Turn it on and DuckDuckGo hides AI-generated images from its image results. It does that with manually curated open-source blocklists, including uBlock Origin's and the uBlacklist Huge AI Blocklist, which list sites known for AI imagery. DuckDuckGo says it won't catch every AI image but will greatly reduce how many you see. There's also a separate No-AI version of the search engine at noai.duckduckgo.com, where the image filter is on by default and DuckDuckGo's other AI features are removed.

**Slop Mop** is a browser extension that reads the posts in your feed before you reach them, on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Facebook, Substack and Medium, and judges the articles you read and the emails in Gmail and Outlook too. An AI model designed for calibrated decisions answers twelve questions about each post's text: nine tells of slop and three counter-signals (sounds like a person, useful to readers, reader response). Likely slop folds into a one-line strip you can reopen, or, in Highlight mode, is marked and left visible. [How it works](/how-it-works.html) explains the score.

## DuckDuckGo's AI image filter vs Slop Mop: where they differ

**Images or words.** DuckDuckGo's filter is about images. Slop Mop reads text and doesn't judge images at all. If your complaint is a page of uncanny AI pictures when you search for "otter", DuckDuckGo's filter is the right tool and Slop Mop won't help.

**Search or reading.** The filter works inside DuckDuckGo image search, in any browser and in DuckDuckGo's apps, including on your phone. Slop Mop works on desktop, in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and other Chromium browsers, on six social networks, the articles and websites you read, and Gmail and Outlook. It doesn't touch search results pages or work on phones.

**Lists or judgement.** A blocklist is fast, transparent and cheap to run, and it's good at the obvious cases: whole sites devoted to generated images. It can't judge an image from a site that isn't on the list, and it only knows what the list maintainers have added. Slop Mop scores every post individually, which lets it catch a first-time poster but means each call is a judgement that can be wrong. That's why it shows its reasons and lets your vote overrule it.

**AI or not.** DuckDuckGo's filter is aimed squarely at AI-generated images. Slop Mop isn't an AI detector: it folds empty writing whoever wrote it, and leaves useful AI-assisted posts alone.

**Settings.** DuckDuckGo's filter is a single switch. Slop Mop has Hide and Highlight modes, three sensitivity levels (Mild, Moderate, Aggressive), a per-post breakdown called the Slopprint, and a vote on each post.

**Price.** DuckDuckGo's filter is free. Slop Mop is free for one feed with 250 checks a day; Slop Mop XL, for every feed, the sites you read, Gmail and Outlook, is US$5.99 a month or US$59.99 a year.

## When to use DuckDuckGo's filter

- AI-generated images keep turning up when you search for pictures.
- You already use DuckDuckGo, or you're happy to switch search engines.
- You want a free fix with nothing to install.
- You want a search engine with its AI features removed (use the No-AI version).

## When to use Slop Mop

- The slop you mind is written: hooks, [engagement bait](/patterns/engagement-bait/), hype and filler in your feeds, your inbox or the articles you land on.
- You want it caught whether a person or AI wrote it.
- You want to see why a post was folded and overrule it if you disagree.

## Using both

There's no overlap, so use both if both problems bother you. DuckDuckGo's switch takes a moment to flip, and Slop Mop runs in the background once you've picked a feed, a mode and a sensitivity level.

One thing neither tool does is deal with AI images in your social feed. DuckDuckGo's filter stops at its own search results, and Slop Mop reads only the words of a post. A Facebook or LinkedIn post with a generated picture and a useful paragraph underneath will be judged by Slop Mop on the paragraph. Generated pictures are part of the wider problem of [AI slop](/glossary/ai-slop/), and for now you'll need another tool for them.
