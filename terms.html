The free plan

Free cleans one feed of your choice, plus 50 checks a day anywhere else. You can change your free feed once every 30 days. What the free plan includes may change; if it gets smaller, we'll say so on this site first.

Slop Mop XL

XL is a subscription: $6.99 a month or $59.99 a year, plus any sales tax or VAT where you live, shown before you pay. It raises the daily limit to the number shown in your account, and includes each new feed and site as it ships. It works in up to five browsers signed in to your account.

Polar is the merchant of record

XL is sold through Polar, which acts as the merchant of record: Polar takes the payment, charges and pays the tax, and sends your receipts and invoices. Your purchase is also covered by Polar's terms, and the charge on your statement may name Polar. Slop Mop never sees or stores your card details.

Automatic renewal

XL renews automatically at the end of each month or year, and Polar charges the card on file, until you cancel. If the price changes, we'll email you before your next renewal, and the new price applies from that renewal on. If a renewal payment fails, Polar retries it for a short while; XL ends if it can't be collected.

Cancelling

Cancel any time: open Slop Mop, go to Account settings, and choose Manage billing (Polar's customer portal). You can also email hello@slopmop.lol. Cancelling stops the next renewal; XL keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, then your account goes back to the free plan. Nothing is deleted.

Refunds

If XL isn't for you, email hello@slopmop.lol within 14 days of a charge and we'll refund that charge in full, no questions asked. After 14 days, payments aren't refunded, but you can cancel so you aren't charged again. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Polar. Where the law where you live gives you more, you get more.

Scores are opinions

Slop Mop's scores are an automated opinion about the writing on a page, never about the person who wrote it. They can be wrong. Don't use them to make decisions about people, such as hiring.
