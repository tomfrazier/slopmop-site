one last step

Your mop can reach everywhere.

To mop more than one feed, Chrome needs your OK for Slop Mop to read the pages you visit. It reads post and article text to score it, and never stores it.

  1. Click the Slop Mop icon in your browser's toolbar. If you can't see it, open the puzzle-piece menu and pin it.
  2. Choose “Let Slop Mop access all sites” and approve Chrome's prompt.
  3. Open any feed. XL is ready on every feed and site. Articles and comments are scored when you click their mop, so checks are only spent on what you ask about.

You can turn site access off again in Chrome's extension settings. Questions about your subscription: hello@slopmop.lol. How billing, renewal and refunds work: terms of service.
