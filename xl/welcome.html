one last step

Your mop can reach everywhere.

To mop more than one feed, your browser needs your OK for Slop Mop to read the pages you visit. It reads post and article text to score it, and never stores it.

Slop Mop opens and asks your browser for you.

Slop Mop in this browser isn't signed in. Click the mop in your toolbar and sign in with the email you used at checkout; XL turns on by itself.

Couldn't reach Slop Mop from this page. Do it from the toolbar instead:

  1. Click the Slop Mop icon in your browser's toolbar. If you can't see it, open the puzzle-piece menu and pin it.
  2. Choose “Let Slop Mop access all sites” and approve your browser's prompt.
  3. Open any feed. XL is ready on every feed and site. Articles and comments are scored when you click their mop, so checks are only spent on what you ask about.

You can turn site access off again in your browser's extension settings. Questions about your subscription: hello@slopmop.lol. How billing, renewal and refunds work: terms of service.
