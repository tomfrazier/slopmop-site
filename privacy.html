Privacy policy

Slop Mop is a research project. The extension reads the posts your own LinkedIn session already rendered, sends the text out to be judged, and keeps as little as it can afterwards. Your account holds your email address, settings and votes. This site uses Google Analytics, and Slop Mop XL is billed through Polar. This page says exactly what all of that means.

The server

All judging requests go to one host: api.slopmop.lol. Apart from Google, if you choose to sign in with it, nothing else is contacted by the extension. The server is run privately by Artimovo LLC. Your account, this website and paid subscriptions are covered further down this page.

What leaves your browser

The post text and its public engagement counts are sent to api.slopmop.lol, and the text is passed on from there to a third-party AI model designed for calibrated decisions, which produces the answers behind the score. Your name is never sent, and neither is the author's.

With Slop Mop XL, and only after you give Chrome permission to read the pages you visit, Slop Mop can also score articles, comments and reviews on other sites. That text is sent the same way, only when you click its mop, and is never stored.

What is stored

For judging, five things, and nothing else:

A hash of the post texta one-way hash, so the same post isn't judged twice. The text itself is not kept.
ScoresThe model's answers to the twelve questions, the slop score, and when the post was first seen.
Engagement countsthe public reaction and comment numbers the usefulness check reads.
A salted hash of the install idenough to count installs and dedupe votes, not enough to identify you.
Votesyour No, Maybe or Probably on a post, kept with your account so your overrides follow you, and only ever counted in aggregate. Votes are never shown to anyone or sent to the author.

No LinkedIn profile data, no browsing history, no cookies set by the extension, and no analytics or advertising vendors in the extension. Usage is capped at 250 checks per install per day.

Your account

Slop Mop uses an account, so your settings, votes and the feed you picked follow you to up to five browsers. You sign in with Google or with a one-time link sent to your email. There is no password.

Your account holds your email address, an account id, your plan, the feed you picked, your settings and your votes. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and the basic profile it shares, such as your name, and Google knows you signed in to Slop Mop. We get no access to your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

Your account is never attached to a post's score, and nothing about you is sent with the text being judged. Email hello@slopmop.lol to have your account and its votes deleted.

Payments for Slop Mop XL

Slop Mop XL is a paid subscription. Polar (Polar Software, Inc.) is the merchant of record: it sells the subscription to you, takes the payment, works out and collects sales tax or VAT, and sends receipts. Polar and the payment processors it works with collect and process your payment data, such as your name, email address, billing address and card or other payment details, under Polar's privacy policy.

Checkout runs on this website, using Polar's checkout, or on Polar's own page. When you upgrade, our server starts the checkout with your account id and email address so the subscription is linked to your account. Polar tells us when it starts, renews, changes or ends.

We never see or store your full card number. From Polar we receive what we need to run your subscription: your email address, name, country, plan, subscription status, and the dates and amounts of payments and refunds. We use it to turn Slop Mop XL on and off for your account, answer billing questions, and keep the records the law requires.

The free plan involves no payment and nothing is sent to Polar.

This website and Google Analytics

This website, slopmop.lol, uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and collects information such as the pages you view, the site that sent you, your browser and device type, and your approximate location, which Google derives from your IP address. Google processes that data under its own policies. We don't use it for advertising and we don't combine it with anything the extension sends.

You can block it with your browser's cookie or tracking settings, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.

The site's fonts are served by Google Fonts. If you sign up for updates, your email address is collected through Formspree and used only to send you Slop Mop news. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Google Analytics runs on this website only. The extension does not load it, and nothing from your feed is sent to Google Analytics.

Retention

Check logs — the operational record of requests hitting the server — are kept for 90 days and then deleted. Your account is kept until you ask us to delete it. Payment and subscription records from Polar are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually seven years. Google Analytics data is kept under Google Analytics' retention setting, at most 14 months. You can unsubscribe from updates at any time, or ask us to delete your address.
