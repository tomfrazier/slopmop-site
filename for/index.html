By role

Slop Mop for CEOs

For chief executives: a gentle setup that folds vendor bait and peer platitudes while keeping posts from your own staff and customers.

Slop Mop for communications and PR teams

Keep every mention in view with the filler marked, and learn which tells make executive posts read as slop.

Slop Mop for consultants

Keep client signals visible and the framework filler marked, so a consultant's LinkedIn scroll turns up something billable.

Slop Mop for creators

For people who build an audience on LinkedIn: fold the growth-hack posts, read the craft, and check your own posts for the tells.

Slop Mop for developers

Fold the vibe-coding boasts and 'developers are finished' takes so a developer's LinkedIn feed shows releases, write-ups and jobs.

Slop Mop for educators

Fold away edtech hype and inspiration posts so teachers and lecturers see classroom practice, resources and jobs on LinkedIn.

Slop Mop for founders

For startup founders: fold away the build-in-public theatre and keep posts from operators who share real numbers and trade-offs.

Slop Mop for freelancers

Fold the income boasts and script giveaways so a freelancer's LinkedIn feed shows the people actually looking to hire.

Slop Mop for hiring managers

For people who hire into their own team: fold away leadership clichés and keep the posts from peers and candidates who know the work.

Slop Mop for investors

For investors and VCs: mark the hype in founder and market posts while keeping every post visible for deal flow and portfolio news.

Slop Mop for job-seekers

For people looking for work: mark the career-advice bait and keep job posts, hiring updates and useful advice in view.

Slop Mop for journalists

Mark the press-release padding and viral hooks on LinkedIn so a reporter can spot the posts with names, dates and facts in them.

Slop Mop for marketers

For marketers: mark the formula posts in your feed, see which tells each one uses, and check your own posts before readers do.

Slop Mop for product managers

Fold the framework threads and 'PMs who don't do X' takes so a product manager's feed shows launches, metrics and post-mortems.

Slop Mop for recruiters

How recruiters can use Slop Mop to keep candidate posts visible while the recruiting thought-leadership fades into the background.

Slop Mop for researchers

Fold away the 'this study changes everything' posts so a researcher's LinkedIn feed shows papers, datasets and methods first.

Slop Mop for sales teams

For sellers and SDRs: fold the sales-influencer bait and keep the prospect and customer posts that tell you who to call.

Slop Mop for small business owners

Fold the growth hacks and AI tool pitches so a small business owner's short LinkedIn check shows customers, suppliers and local news.
