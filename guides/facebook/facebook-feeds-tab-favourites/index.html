How to use the Feeds tab and Favorites on Facebook

Open Facebook's Feeds tab, add up to 30 Favorites, and pin it to your tab bar so a feed without suggestions is one tap away.

6 min read · steps checked 18 october 2026

on this page Before you start 1. Open the Facebook Feeds tab 2. Pick a filter 3. Add Favorites 4. Other ways into Favorites (app) 5. Pin Feeds to your tab bar What the Facebook Feeds tab changes, and what it doesn't If that doesn't work

The Feeds tab is Facebook's other feed: recent posts from the people, Pages and groups you follow, newest first, with no personalised ranking and no suggested posts (Facebook Help). Inside it, Favorites narrows things further to up to 30 friends and Pages you pick. Neither replaces your main feed for good, so the trick is to pin Feeds where you'll actually use it.

Facebook spells the feature "Favorites", so that's the label you'll see on screen.

Before you start

  • It resets. When you exit and reopen the app, Facebook puts you back on the ranked Home feed (Facebook Help). There's no setting to make Feeds your default.
  • Favorites has limits. Up to 30 friends and Pages, no groups, and nobody is told when you add or remove them (Facebook Help).
  • Recent means everything. Chronological order shows every post from everyone you follow, including the dull ones the ranking would have buried. If Feeds feels noisy, that's a sign to unfollow, which a later guide covers.

1. Open the Facebook Feeds tab

In the app (iPhone and Android):

  1. Tap the menu at the top left of Facebook.
  2. Tap Feeds, under All shortcuts.

On a computer:

  1. Go to your Home feed.
  2. Click Feeds in the left-hand menu. If it isn't shown, click See more.

These are the steps in Facebook's help pages (Facebook Help). If you use Feeds often, Facebook may pin it to your main navigation by itself.

2. Pick a filter

Inside Feeds you can switch between four views (Facebook Help):

FilterWhat it shows
FavoritesPosts and reels from the friends and Pages you've favourited
FriendsPosts and reels from your friends
GroupsPosts and reels from groups you've joined or run
PagesPosts and reels from Pages you like or follow

The Groups filter is the quickest way to catch up on the groups you joined for a reason (the allotment, the school, the running club) without the main feed's guesses mixed in.

3. Add Favorites

Favorites are moved higher in your main feed as well as getting their own filter, and you'll see their newest posts first (Facebook Help). If you once set people to "See First", Facebook has already moved them here.

In the app:

  1. Tap the menu, then Feeds.
  2. Tap Favorites, then Manage Favorites.
  3. Search for a friend or Page and add them, or tap Add next to Facebook's suggestions.
  4. To remove one, tap the three dots next to them, then Remove [Name] From Favorites.

On a computer:

  1. Click your profile picture at the top right.
  2. Click Settings & privacy, then Content preferences.
  3. Click Favorites. Click Favorite next to a friend or Page to add them, or Remove Favorite to take them off.
  4. Click Exit when you're done.

(Facebook Help)

Who to pick

Thirty is fewer than it sounds, which helps. Choose the people whose posts you'd be sorry to miss: close family, a handful of friends, the local council or school Page, a business you buy from. Leave out anyone you favourite out of guilt; you can still see them in the Friends filter.

4. Other ways into Favorites (app)

Facebook's help page lists three more routes in the app (Facebook Help):

  • On your feed, tap Filter and then Favorites.
  • Tap the menu, then Saved (you may need See more), then Feed and Favorites.
  • Search for "Favorites" and tap it in the results.

The desktop route is simpler: Feeds in the left-hand menu, then Favorites. Click All to go back to everything.

5. Pin Feeds to your tab bar

Because the app always reopens on Home, the most useful step is making Feeds one tap away. This only works in the iPhone and Android apps (Facebook Help).

  1. Tap the menu, then Settings & privacy.
  2. Tap Settings, then Tab bar.
  3. Tap Customize the bar.
  4. Use the drop-down next to a shortcut to choose Pin, Auto or Hide.

Facebook's help page doesn't list every shortcut you can pin, so if Feeds isn't offered on your account, pin the nearest one you have. While you're there, hiding shortcuts you never use (and turning off notification dots by pressing and holding a tab) cuts some of the nagging.

The Friends tab, if you have it

In March 2025 Meta relaunched a Friends tab in the US and Canada that shows only content from your Facebook friends, with no recommended posts, and said you could pin it from the same Tab bar setting (Meta). If you're outside those countries it may not appear.

What the Facebook Feeds tab changes, and what it doesn't

Feeds removes the suggested posts and the ranking, which is most of what people mean when they say Facebook used to be better. It doesn't filter what your friends, Pages and groups post themselves. A group you joined in 2019 that now posts nothing but "Type YES if you remember this!" will be right there in date order.

Only real 80s kids will know what this is 👇 Type YES if you remember!

Both could turn up in your Groups filter, and only the second one asks for something useful. The first is engagement bait, and date order gives it the same space as the lost cat.

If that doesn't work

  • Feeds isn't in your menu. Check See more on desktop or scroll the shortcuts list in the app. Some Facebook features reach accounts gradually, and the menu may be labelled differently on yours.
  • The main feed hasn't changed. It won't. Favorites raises a few people in it, and the suggested posts guide covers the rest. Facebook says the main feed returns to its default ranking even after you sort it (Facebook Help).
  • You keep forgetting to use it. Pin it, then move the app to a different place on your phone so muscle memory doesn't open Home for you.
