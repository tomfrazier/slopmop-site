How to mute a writer or publication on Medium

Mute a Medium writer or publication from the three-dot menu, see what muting hides, and find your muted list to undo it.

5 min read · steps checked 18 october 2026

Open the three-dot menu on any story preview from the writer or publication and choose to mute them; you can also do it from the three-dot menu on their profile page. Their stories leave your homepage feed and your email digests, and they aren't told. You can unmute at any time from the Muted tab on the Refine recommendations page.

When to mute a writer or publication on Medium

Show less like this is a nudge about one story. Muting is a firm rule about a source. Reach for it when one account keeps turning up with the same kind of story:

  • a writer who publishes several near-identical listicles a day under different titles
  • a publication whose stories are mostly adverts for a course or a newsletter
  • an account whose every headline promises income, followers or a new life with no cost attached, the long-form version of no-catch promises
  • a writer who ends every piece by asking you to clap 50 times, follow and subscribe, which is engagement bait in a different costume

If the problem is a whole subject rather than one source, mute the topic instead. Medium added topic muting in May 2026, and the recommendations clean-up guide covers it.

Unfollowing and muting do different jobs. Unfollowing removes a source from the stories you asked for. Muting also keeps it out of recommendations, which matters when a writer is popular in topics you follow.

1. Mute from a story preview

This is the quickest way, and it works from your feed.

  1. On your Medium homepage, find a story by the writer or publication.
  2. Click the three-dot button on the story preview.
  3. Choose the option to mute the writer, or the option to mute the publication. Medium's labels have read "Mute this author" and "Mute this publication"; the wording may differ slightly in your version.
  4. Refresh the page (or relaunch the app). Medium's help page says existing stories from that source are removed from your feed after a refresh or relaunch.

A story published by a writer inside a publication gives you both options. Pick the one that matches the problem. If one writer is the issue, mute the writer and you'll keep seeing the rest of the publication. If the publication publishes a lot of filler from many writers, mute the publication.

2. Mute from a profile page

Use this when you've landed on a writer's or publication's page and decided you've seen enough.

  1. Open the writer's profile or the publication's homepage.
  2. Click the three-dot button on the page.
  3. Choose mute.

The steps are the same in the app and on desktop, though the three-dot button may sit in a different spot on a phone screen.

What muting does

According to Medium's help page and its launch post for the feature:

  • Stories from the muted writer or publication stop appearing on your homepage.
  • They stop appearing in your Medium email digests.
  • The writer or publication isn't notified.
  • You can still visit their profile or homepage and read them directly.
  • You can still follow a muted writer, though their stories won't be recommended to you in your feeds.

What muting doesn't do

Muting is a personal filter. It doesn't report anyone, remove their stories from Medium, or stop other readers seeing them. If a story breaks Medium's rules (spam, harassment, plagiarism), that's a matter for Medium's reporting process, which is separate from muting.

Muting also won't catch a story that someone else shares with you directly, or one that ranks in a search engine. It only filters what Medium recommends to you.

3. Find your muted list and unmute

People mute in a hurry and forget. If a writer you liked seems to have disappeared, check here first.

  1. On desktop, click your profile picture in the top right corner.
  2. Open Settings, then Refine recommendations.
  3. Choose the Muted tab. It lists the writers and publications you've muted.
  4. Remove the ones you want back.

Medium's help pages also describe a muted writers and publications section in account settings. Either route reaches the same list, and the app may label it slightly differently.

A simple muting habit

Muting works best as a rule with a threshold, so it doesn't turn into a grudge list. One that works for many readers is three strikes: if a source gives you three stories in a row that fail the two-minute check, mute it. That's enough evidence to be fair, and few enough strikes that the feed improves within a week or two.

Run through the muted list every few months. Writers change, publications change editors, and a source that was all filler in spring may be worth another look by winter.

If that doesn't work

If muted stories still show up:

  • Refresh the page or relaunch the app; Medium says removal happens after a refresh.
  • Check that you muted the right thing. Writers and publications are muted separately, so muting one leaves the other in place.
  • Check whether the stories arrive through a topic you follow. If many different writers produce the same kind of filler under one topic, mute or unfollow the topic.

Muting is the most precise tool Medium gives you for sources you've already judged.
