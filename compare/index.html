Slop Mop compared

How Slop Mop differs from ad blockers, spam filters, AI detectors, mute lists and the other tools people use to cut the noise, checked and dated. Each one does a different job.

Compared with other approaches

Slop Mop vs ad blockers: what a slop filter adds

Ad blockers remove paid placements and trackers; Slop Mop judges the organic posts that remain. Different jobs, and they run side by side.

Slop Mop vs AI detectors

AI detectors estimate whether AI wrote a text. Slop Mop judges whether the writing is worth your time, whoever or whatever wrote it.

Slop Mop vs browser reading modes and AI summarisers

Reading modes and summarisers tidy or shorten what you've opened; Slop Mop screens a feed first and folds what isn't worth opening.

Slop Mop vs feed blockers and hiders

Feed blockers remove the feed to break the scrolling habit; Slop Mop keeps the feed and folds only the posts it judges low-value.

Slop Mop vs keyword and mute-word filters

Keyword filters hide posts that contain words you choose; Slop Mop judges the whole post's writing and needs no word list.

Slop Mop vs LinkedIn's own feed controls

LinkedIn's controls act after you've read a post and shape its algorithm over time; Slop Mop judges each post first and shows why.

Slop Mop vs newsletter unsubscribe and email triage tools

Email triage tools act on whole senders; Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook judges the writing of each message you keep.

Slop Mop vs RSS and feed readers

Feed readers let you leave the algorithm for hand-picked sources; Slop Mop keeps you on the platform and judges each post's writing.

Slop Mop vs spam filters

Spam filters catch junk, scams and phishing at the server; Slop Mop judges legitimate mail and posts on their writing. One doesn't replace the other.

Slop Mop vs unfollowing and muting people

Muting removes everything a person posts, good or bad; Slop Mop judges each post on its writing, so the good ones still get through.

Compared with other products

Slop Mop vs Cringe Guard

Two filters for low-value posts: Cringe Guard runs an LLM on your API key on LinkedIn, Slop Mop scores nine tells across feeds with no key.

Slop Mop vs DeSlop

Two slop filters: DeSlop hunts signs of AI writing on your device, Slop Mop judges whether a post is worth reading.

Slop Mop vs DuckDuckGo's AI image filter

DuckDuckGo's setting hides AI images in search results; Slop Mop folds low-value written posts in your feeds, inbox and the sites you read.

Slop Mop vs GPTZero

GPTZero checks documents for AI authorship on demand; Slop Mop filters low-value posts from your feed as you scroll.

Slop Mop vs Kagi SlopStop

Kagi SlopStop cleans AI content farms out of search results; Slop Mop folds low-value posts in your feeds and judges the pages you read.

Slop Mop vs News Feed Eradicator: an alternative that keeps the feed

News Feed Eradicator removes the whole feed so you stop scrolling. Slop Mop keeps the feed and folds only the posts it judges low-value.

Slop Mop vs Originality.ai

Originality.ai checks content before you publish it; Slop Mop screens what other people post in your feeds and inbox.

Slop Mop vs Pangram

Pangram labels text as human, AI-assisted or AI; Slop Mop judges whether a post is worth reading and can fold it away.

Slop Mop vs SaneBox: a different kind of SaneBox alternative

SaneBox sorts email by sender and importance on any account; Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook judges whether the writing is worth reading.

Slop Mop vs Slop Evader

Slop Evader hides everything after November 2022 from your searches. Slop Mop reads current posts and keeps the useful ones.

Slop Mop vs Social Fixer

Social Fixer filters Facebook by rules you write. Slop Mop judges how each post is written, on Facebook and other feeds.

Slop Mop vs uBlock Origin

uBlock Origin blocks ads and trackers by filter list. Slop Mop reads the posts between them and folds the low-value ones.

Slop Mop vs Unhook

Unhook switches off whole parts of YouTube. Slop Mop reads feed posts one at a time and folds the low-value ones.

Slop Mop vs Unroll.Me: an alternative that reads the email

Unroll.Me sorts newsletters by sender, free, funded by commercial-email data. Slop Mop for Gmail and Outlook judges the writing.

Fold the slop out of your feed

Slop Mop reads your feeds, the sites you read and your inbox ahead of you, folds away the slop and shows its working. Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Free for one feed, 250 checks a day.
