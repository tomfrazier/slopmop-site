Which patterns show up most in LinkedIn posts

Counter-signals (higher is better)Tells of slop
  1. Useful to readers47%Counter-signal: the post gives the reader something they can use. Higher is better.
  2. Sounds like a person44%Counter-signal: a specific, human voice. Higher is better.
  3. Empty praise34%Calls something meaningful or profound and never says what it did.
  4. “Not X, but Y”31%Defines things by what they aren't, so every line sounds like a revelation.
  5. Scroll-stopper18%A dramatic one-line opener built to stop the thumb.
  6. Flowery prose16%Flourish in place of the plain claim.
  7. Too-tidy story14%An anecdote that exists only to land on its lesson.
  8. Engagement bait14%Asks for the reaction instead of earning it.
  9. Hype words12%Marketing vocabulary standing in for a claim.
  10. Stiff phrasing8%Stock transitions and hedges that say less.
  11. No-catch promises5%All benefit, no cost.
