What stood out

  1. Praise without a point leads. Empty praise was the strongest tell all month: words like “meaningful” and “transformative” with nothing behind them.
  2. The counter-signals hold up. Usefulness and a human voice averaged higher than any single tell. Most of the feed isn't slop; the slop is just loud.
  3. Engagement bait ticked up in the last week, mostly “comment YES” posts.
