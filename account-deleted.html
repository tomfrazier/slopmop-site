Your Slop Mop account is deleted

That's done. Your account and the personal data in it are gone, and you've been signed out.

What we deleted

  • Your email address, sign-in and sessions
  • Your plan, settings, email settings and dashboard history
  • The link between your account and your browsers and email add-ons

If you had Slop Mop XL

Your subscription is cancelled and you won't be charged again. Polar, which handles our billing, has deleted your customer profile and keeps only the order records tax law requires. Your receipts from Polar still work as proof of payment. Billing questions: hello@slopmop.lol.

What's left

  • Your votes, in the anonymous tallies. They were stored so they can't be traced to you, and now nothing links them to you at all.
  • Slop Mop itself. The extension and any email add-ons are still installed, now linked to no one. Remove them if you're done (below), or keep going: you can sign up again any time with the same email, starting fresh.
  • Short-lived logs that don't identify you, deleted within 90 days. The privacy policy has the details.

Changed your mind?

We can't bring a deleted account back, but you can make a new one in a minute: open Slop Mop's popup and sign in.

Removing Slop Mop

Chrome, Edge and Chromium browsersRight-click the Slop Mop icon in your toolbar and choose Remove from Chrome (in Edge, Remove from Microsoft Edge; in Comet, Brave or Opera, Remove).
FirefoxOpen the menu (☰), choose Add-ons and themes → Extensions, then … next to Slop Mop and Remove.
SafariOpen Safari → Settings → Extensions, select Slop Mop and choose Uninstall.
GmailIn Gmail, open Slop Mop in the side panel, click its More menu (⋮) and choose Manage add-on → Uninstall. To remove its access to your Google account as well, go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Slop Mop.
OutlookIn new Outlook or Outlook on the web, open More apps → Add apps → Manage your apps, then choose … next to Slop Mop and Remove. In classic Outlook, use Home → All Apps (or Get Add-ins) → My add-ins.
